Featured story

Watershed development

  • Watershed programmes in 14 districts of Tamil Nadu
  • Since 2000

A check dam holds a pool of water at the head of a field. Structures like this one slow the run-off after rain so that it soaks into the ground instead of carrying the topsoil away. Coodu Trust works on watershed schemes as a coordinating agency, consultant and training partner to the district agencies that implement them — mobilising village committees, running participatory appraisals, and training watershed development teams.
