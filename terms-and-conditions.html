1. Who we are

This website, coodutrust.org, is owned and operated by COODU TRUST, a public charitable trust constituted by Trust Deed dated 20 June 2000, registered as Document No. 150/B IV of 2000 with the Sub-Registrar, Dindigul, as amended by supplementary deeds dated 24 February 2006 (Doc. No. 92/BK4/2006) and 15 March 2007 (Doc. No. 156/BK4 of 2007). Our PAN is AAATC5133E.

COODU TRUST works on rural development across Tamil Nadu — watershed development, sustainable agriculture, women’s empowerment and self-help groups, education and skilling, health and sanitation, solid waste management, and consultancy and HR services.

2. What this site is for

This site exists to tell you about our work and to let you support it with a donation. It is an information and donation site. There is no account to create, no login, no subscription, and nothing on this site is for sale.

4. Donations are gifts, not purchases

A donation made through this site is a voluntary charitable contribution to COODU TRUST.

It is not a payment for goods or services. You receive no product, no service, no membership, no entitlement and no benefit in return. Nothing is being bought or sold, and consumer protections that apply to the purchase of goods or services do not apply to a gift.

Where you choose a cause on the donation form, we treat that as an indication of your preference and apply your gift to that area of our work so far as it is practicable to do so. Trustees remain responsible under the trust deed for applying funds to the Trust’s charitable objects, and may reallocate a contribution where a programme is fully funded, has closed, or where circumstances in the field require it.

By making a donation you confirm that:

6. This facility is for donations from Indian sources

Donations through this website are accepted in Indian rupees (INR) only.

Contributions from foreign sources are governed in India by the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010, which requires them to be received into a specifically designated bank account and not through a general payment facility of this kind. This donation form is therefore intended for donations from Indian sources.

If you are a foreign national, a foreign entity, or otherwise a “foreign source” under the FCRA, please do not use this form. Write to director@coodutrust.org first and we will tell you the correct route.

If we return a donation, we will return it to the original payment method.

9. Receipts and Section 80G

COODU TRUST holds approval under Section 80G of the Income-tax Act, 1961, granted by order C.No.102/110/CIT-II/2006-07 dated 28 May 2009.

Approvals under Section 80G were brought under a revised registration framework in 2020, under which approvals run for limited periods and must be renewed. Whether you can actually claim a deduction for a particular donation depends on the Trust’s approval being valid on the date of your donation, on the donation being reported correctly, and on your own tax position in the relevant year.

We can issue a receipt on request, but we do not and cannot guarantee any tax outcome for you. If you intend to claim a deduction, please confirm the current status of our approval with us before you give, and take your own tax advice. Nothing on this website is tax advice.

A deduction under Section 80G reduces your taxable income by a proportion of the amount given, subject to the limits in the Act. It is not an exemption of the full amount, and the proportion is not the same for every donor or every donation.

10. The impact figures on the donation page are estimates

Our donation page shows equivalences of the “₹500 ≈ 25 saplings” kind, and similar illustrations appear elsewhere on the site.

These are illustrative modelled estimates — not guarantees, not commitments, and not audited figures. They rest on our own average cost assumptions, which change with prices, seasons, locations, partners and project design. Giving ₹500 does not create a contractual obligation on us to plant 25 saplings, or to spend your particular rupees on that particular activity.

They are there to give you a feel for what money does in our work. They are not a specification of what your donation buys, and no warranty of any kind is given in respect of them.
