Our Programs of Impact

Comprehensive development approach across five key areas of community transformation

Transforming Communities Through Integrated Development

Since 2000, Coodu Trust has been working across five interconnected sectors to create sustainable change in rural Tamil Nadu. Our holistic approach ensures that communities develop comprehensively, addressing immediate needs while building long-term resilience.

🌾

Agriculture & Livelihood Enhancement

Improving farm-based income and strengthening the rural economy

Watershed Development Programs

2001-2004

Comprehensive watershed management across 9 districts under DPAP, IWDP, TNCWP, and NWDPRA schemes, focusing on soil and water conservation, and sustainable agriculture.

Key Activities:

  • Management of 248 watersheds across 9 districts
  • Rainwater harvesting and groundwater recharge
  • Land development and reclamation programs
  • Construction of check dams and percolation tanks
  • Social forestry and orchard plantations
248 Watersheds Managed
9 Districts Covered

Integrated Farming Systems

2010-2011

Demonstration model integrating agroforestry, horticulture, dairy, mushroom cultivation, and vermicompost units to showcase sustainable farming practices.

Key Activities:

  • Integrated farming demonstration in Karur district
  • Agroforestry and horticulture integration
  • Dairy farming with backyard poultry
  • Mushroom cultivation and vermicompost production
  • Post-harvest management training
50 Vermicompost Units
130 Commercial Farms

Skill Development & Training Programs

2004-2014

Extensive training programs on organic farming, livestock management, and modern agricultural techniques to enhance farmer capabilities and productivity.

Key Activities:

  • Training on organic farming for 2,345 farmers
  • Scientific goat farming training for 1,775 farm women
  • Backyard poultry training for 935 marginal farmers
  • Vermicompost technology training for 1,367 farmers
  • Farm Field Schools on major crops
5,000+ Farmers Trained
200+ Training Programs

Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)

2015-2023

Promoting FPOs across Dindigul and Thiruvarur districts, focusing on collective farming, marketing, and value chain development for sustainable agriculture.

Key Activities:

  • Formation and strengthening of 4 FPOs
  • Enrollment of 1,082 farmers in FPOs
  • Collective procurement and marketing
  • Training on FPO management for 600 farmers
  • Focus on hill garlic, maize, paddy, and groundnut
1,082 Enrolled Farmers
4 Active FPOs

Women Farmer Empowerment Programs

2019-2021

Mahila Kisan Sashaktikaran Pariyojana (MKSP) empowering women farmers through training in sustainable agriculture and animal husbandry practices.

Key Activities:

  • MKSP program empowering 5,000 women farmers
  • Training 2,134 women in Organic Agriculture
  • Formation of 100 Joint Liability Groups (JLGs)
  • Women-led agricultural enterprises
  • Sustainable farming practices promotion
5,000 Women Empowered
100 JLGs Formed

Dairy Development Projects

2021-2023

Sustainable livelihood enhancement through scientific management of dairy animals, including formation of Tharangampatti Dairy Farmers Producer Company and milk collection centers.

Key Activities:

  • Formation of Tharangampatti Dairy Farmers Producer Company
  • Training 260 women farmers in scientific dairy management
  • Establishment of 10 milk collection centers
  • Daily handling of 1,800 litres of milk
  • State Balanced Growth Fund project implementation
500 Women Farmers
1,800 Litres Daily

Livestock & Dairy Development

Income Generation

Comprehensive training on dairy farming, goat rearing, and backyard poultry to enhance productivity and income, including Joint Livelihood Groups (JLGs).

Key Activities:

  • Dairy farming and goat rearing training
  • Backyard poultry development
  • Joint Livelihood Groups formation
  • NABARD partnership for financial access
1,000+ Livestock Farmers
25% Yield Increase

Skilling & Employment

Vocational training and entrepreneurship for improved job prospects

Government Skill Development Programs

PIA Status

Project Implementing Agency for DDU-GKY, NULM, NRuM, TAHDCO, and PMKVY schemes, training hundreds of rural youth with 90%+ placement rates.

Key Programs:

  • Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana
  • National Urban Livelihoods Mission
  • National Rurban Mission training
  • TAHDCO training for SC women
90%+ Placement Rate
1,000+ Youth Trained

Multi-Trade

Wide array of courses including Sewing Machine Operation, Mobile Repairing, Multi-Cuisine Cook, Data Entry, supported by partners like NIFT-TEA and Cheran Spinning Mills.

Key Activities:

  • Apparel & Textiles: Sewing Machine Operator
  • IT & Office Skills: Tally, Computer Skills
  • Technical Trades: Mobile Repair, Tractor Operation
  • Service Trades: Beautician, Hotel Management
25+ Different Trades
500+ Women Trained

Training individuals to start small businesses in mushroom cultivation, paper bag making, value-added food products, and other micro-enterprises.

Environment & Water Management

Natural resource management and environmental conservation

Rainwater Harvesting & Watershed Management

Flagship Program

Implementing IWMP and PMKSY across 14+ districts, with large-scale CSR projects involving tank desilting, recharge shafts, and farm ponds with partners like TATA Power and ATC Tyres.

Key Activities:

  • Tank desilting and construction of recharge shafts
  • Farm pond creation and water harvesting
  • Integrated Watershed Management Programme
  • CSR partnerships with corporate entities
200+ Crore Liters Water Stored
14+ Districts Covered

Solid Waste Management

Municipal Partnership

Managing complete SWM program for Dindigul City Municipal Corporation, handling 86 tonnes daily with 400 workers, plus innovative ECOMAC programs in Kancheepuram.

Key Activities:

  • Daily waste collection and processing (86 tonnes)
  • Management of 400 sanitary workers
  • ECOMAC decomposition machine implementation
  • Renault Nissan partnership projects
86 Tonnes Daily Waste Handled
400 Sanitary Workers

Environmental Awareness & Greening

Conservation

Consistent tree plantation drives planting tens of thousands of trees (Palmyrah, Mango, etc.) and conducting environmental awareness programs in schools and communities.

Key Activities:

  • Large-scale tree plantation drives
  • School and community awareness programs
  • Partnership with Department of Environment
  • Biodiversity conservation initiatives
50,000+ Trees Planted
100+ Schools Covered
🏥

Health & Sanitation

Improving public health through medical services and water safety

Water Quality & Safety

TWAD Board Partner

Managing and operating 22 water quality testing laboratories across seven districts for TWAD Board, testing over 66,000 samples annually and training 1,200+ community members.

Key Activities:

  • Operating 22 water quality testing labs
  • Annual testing of 66,000+ water samples
  • Field Test Kit (FTK) training under Jal Jeevan Mission
  • Training PRI representatives on water testing
66,000+ Samples Tested Annually
1,200+ Community Members Trained

Community Health Services

Direct Services

Operating Ambulatory & Mortuary Services benefiting hundreds of individuals in emergencies, organizing regular medical camps including specialized COVID-19 response.

Key Activities:

  • 24/7 Ambulatory emergency services
  • Mortuary services for communities
  • Regular medical and health camps
  • COVID-19 specialized health camps
500+ Emergency Services
50+ Health Camps

Sanitation & Disease Prevention

Public Health

Implementing Total Sanitation Campaign (TSC) and Swachh Bharath initiatives, building thousands of household latrines and running DBC Program in Dindigul for mosquito control.

Key Activities:

  • Total Sanitation Campaign implementation
  • Household latrine construction (thousands built)
  • Domestic Breeding Checkers (DBC) Program
  • School sanitation and hygiene awareness
2,500+ Toilets Built
90% Coverage Rate

Community & Social Infrastructure

Building foundational community assets and social safety nets

Education & Village Infrastructure

SAP & VAP Programs

School Adoption Programme (SAP) and Village Adoption Programme (VAP) supported by United Way Mumbai, constructing classrooms, libraries, science labs, toilets, and providing computers.

Key Activities:

  • New classroom and library construction
  • Science lab setup and computer provision
  • School campus renovation and toilets
  • Evening tuition centers for rural students
50+ Schools Adopted
20+ Villages Adopted

Care Centers

Key Activities:

  • Short Stay Home for crisis intervention
  • Old Age Home for elderly care
  • De-addiction center for youth support
  • Counseling and rehabilitation services

SHG & Goshala

  • Goshala operation and dairy unit

  • Consumer rights awareness programs
