Our Programs of Impact

Comprehensive development approach across five key areas of community transformation

Transforming Communities Through Integrated Development

Since 2000, Coodu Trust has been working across six interconnected sectors to create sustainable change in rural Tamil Nadu. Our holistic approach ensures that communities develop comprehensively, addressing immediate needs while building long-term resilience.

🌍

Climate Resilience & Adaptation

Core Focus

Building community resilience to climate change through comprehensive risk assessment, adaptation strategies, and sustainable infrastructure development.

Key Activities:

  • Climate vulnerability assessments
  • Disaster risk reduction planning
  • Early warning systems development
  • Climate-smart infrastructure design
  • Community-based adaptation programs

Watershed Management

Water Conservation

Comprehensive watershed development across 640 micro-watersheds in 24 districts, focusing on soil and water conservation for sustainable agriculture. Coodu Trust is the only empanelled Field Agency for the Ministry of Jal Shakti.

Key Activities:

  • 640 micro-watersheds management across 24 districts
  • Rainwater harvesting and groundwater recharge
  • Check dams and percolation tanks construction
  • Farm pond creation and water storage
  • Soil erosion control measures
640 Micro-Watersheds Managed
24 Districts Covered

Health, Sanitation & Waste Management

Improving public health through medical services, water safety, sanitation, and solid waste management (20 years of dedicated service)

Water Quality & Safety

TWAD Board Partner

Managing and operating 22 water quality testing laboratories across seven districts for TWAD Board, testing over 66,000 samples annually and training 1,200+ community members.

Key Activities:

  • Operating 22 water quality testing labs
  • Annual testing of 66,000+ water samples
  • Field Test Kit (FTK) training under Jal Jeevan Mission
  • Training PRI representatives on water testing
66,000+ Samples Tested Annually
1,200+ Community Members Trained

Community Health Services

Direct Services

Operating Ambulatory & Mortuary Services benefiting hundreds of individuals in emergencies. ATC Tires partnership: 20 health camps with 10 specialist doctors, 20 paramedical staff, treating 9,000+ individuals. Specialized COVID-19 response camps.

Key Activities:

  • 24/7 Ambulatory emergency services
  • Mortuary services for communities (TNHSP)
  • 20 health camps (ATC Tires partnership)
  • 10 specialist doctors and 20 paramedical staff deployment
  • COVID-19 specialized health camps
  • HIV/AIDS awareness programs
9,000+ Individuals Treated
20 Health Camps

Sanitation & Disease Prevention

Public Health

Implementing Total Sanitation Campaign (TSC) in 11 districts and Swachh Bharath initiatives, building 2,500+ household latrines. Running DBC Program and Dengue Eradication Programme in Dindigul for mosquito control and disease prevention.

Key Activities:

  • Total Sanitation Campaign (TSC) in 11 districts
  • Individual and institutional sanitation facilities (2,500+ toilets)
  • Domestic Breeding Checkers (DBC) Program in Dindigul
  • Dengue Eradication Programme implementation
  • School sanitation and hygiene awareness
  • Swachh Bharat Mission initiatives
2,500+ Toilets Built
11 Districts (TSC)

Solid Waste Management

20 Years Experience

20 years of expertise in municipal solid waste management. Managing Dindigul MC (48 wards, 54,000 HH, 90 tons/day, 400 workers, ₹7.1 crore) and Padappai (Renault Nissan, ECO-MAC 2T/day, 70%+ segregation, 3,000 HH).

Key Activities:

  • Dindigul Municipal Corporation: 48 wards, 90 tons/day waste management
  • Padappai SIPCOT: Zero-waste initiative (Renault Nissan partnership)
  • Door-to-door collection (54,000+ households)
  • Source segregation (70%+ efficiency)
  • ECO-MAC composting plant operation (2 tons/day)
  • 400+ sanitation workers management
90 Tons Daily Waste Managed
70%+ Segregation Rate
