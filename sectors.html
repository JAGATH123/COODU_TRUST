Our Sectors of Impact

Comprehensive development approach across five key areas of community transformation

Transforming Communities Through Integrated Development

Since 2000, Coodu Trust has been working across five interconnected sectors to create sustainable change in rural Tamil Nadu. Our holistic approach ensures that communities develop comprehensively, addressing immediate needs while building long-term resilience.

🌱

Natural Resource Management & Sustainable Agriculture

Building resilient agricultural systems and restoring degraded lands

Watershed Development

Major Initiative

Comprehensive watershed management through government programs including DPAP, IWDP, and NWDPRA, transforming drought-prone areas into sustainable agricultural landscapes.

Key Activities:

  • Check dams and percolation ponds construction
  • Farm ponds and water harvesting structures
  • Soil conservation and land treatment
  • Community-based natural resource management
25,000+ Acres Treated
150+ Water Structures

Sustainable Farming Practices

Ongoing

Promoting environmentally friendly farming methods that increase productivity while preserving soil health and reducing input costs.

Key Activities:

  • Organic farming training and certification
  • Vermicomposting and bio-manure production
  • Medicinal plants cultivation
  • Kitchen gardens and nutrition security
3,000+ Farmers Trained
40% Cost Reduction

Livelihood Promotion

Multi-faceted

Diversifying rural livelihoods through innovative agricultural enterprises and value-added activities.

Key Activities:

  • Jatropha cultivation for biofuel production
  • Mushroom cultivation training
  • Horticulture and agro-forestry promotion
  • Value chain development and marketing
1,200+ Enterprises Started
₹15L+ Additional Income
👥

Women Empowerment & Livelihoods

Empowering women as catalysts of social and economic transformation

Self-Help Groups (SHGs)

Core Program

Forming and strengthening women's collectives to build financial independence, leadership skills, and community solidarity.

Key Activities:

  • SHG formation and institutional strengthening
  • Leadership development training
  • Financial literacy and management
  • Microfinance and credit linkages
450+ SHGs Formed
5,000+ Women Members

Micro-Enterprise Development

Livelihood

Supporting women entrepreneurs in establishing and scaling small businesses for sustainable income generation.

Key Activities:

  • Business development training
  • Skill development in various trades
  • Market linkage facilitation
  • Access to credit and technology
800+ Enterprises
300% Income Increase

Gender & Development

Social Change

Addressing gender inequalities through awareness, education, and community mobilization programs.

Key Activities:

  • Legal literacy programs
  • Community dialogue initiatives

Health & Sanitation

Ensuring healthy communities through preventive healthcare and sanitation

HIV/AIDS Prevention

TANSACS Partner

Comprehensive HIV/AIDS prevention and care programs in partnership with TANSACS, focusing on high-risk populations.

Key Activities:

  • Targeted Intervention (TI) for female workers
  • Bridge-and-core-group interventions
  • Counseling and testing services
  • Community awareness programs
15,000+ People Reached
85% Awareness Level

Reproductive & Child Health

RCH Program

Promoting maternal and child health through camps, awareness programs, and healthcare access initiatives.

Key Activities:

  • Maternal health camps and screening
  • Child immunization drives
  • Nutrition awareness programs
  • Adolescent health education
8,000+ Health Checkups
95% Immunization Rate

Water & Sanitation

TSC Initiative

Improving access to safe drinking water and promoting household sanitation under the Total Sanitation Campaign.

Key Activities:

  • Household toilet construction
  • Safe drinking water systems
  • Hygiene education programs
  • Community sanitation initiatives
2,500+ Toilets Built
90% Coverage Rate

Governance

Collective Action
