Volunteer Opportunities

Find the perfect way to contribute based on your skills, interests, and availability

🌾

Field Work & Community Engagement

Work directly with rural communities on watershed development, organic farming, and women's empowerment programs. Perfect for hands-on experience in development work.

Time Commitment: 2-4 weeks minimum
Location: Rural Tamil Nadu villages
Skills Needed: Communication, adaptability, Hindi/Tamil helpful
Best For: Students, gap year, career changers
✓ Accommodation provided ✓ Cultural immersion ✓ Certificate provided
💼

Professional Skills Volunteering

Share your professional expertise in areas like accounting, marketing, IT, legal, or project management to strengthen our organizational capacity.

Time Commitment: 5-10 hours per week
Location: Remote/Online + occasional visits
Skills Needed: Professional expertise in specific areas
Best For: Working professionals, retirees
✓ Flexible timing ✓ Remote work ✓ Skill development
📚

Education & Training

Conduct workshops, training sessions, and educational programs for community members, farmers, and women's groups on various development topics.

Time Commitment: Weekend workshops or week-long programs
Location: Community centers, villages, our office
Skills Needed: Teaching, presentation, subject expertise
Best For: Teachers, trainers, subject experts
✓ Impactful teaching ✓ Material support ✓ Recognition
📱

Digital & Communications

Help with social media, content creation, photography, videography, website management, and digital marketing to amplify our impact.

Time Commitment: 3-5 hours per week
Location: Fully remote
Skills Needed: Digital marketing, design, writing, photography
Best For: Students, freelancers, digital professionals
✓ Portfolio building ✓ Creative freedom ✓ Flexible schedule
🎉

Event Management & Fundraising

Organize fundraising events, awareness campaigns, and community outreach programs to support our mission and expand our reach.

Time Commitment: Project-based, 10-20 hours total
Location: Urban centers, colleges, offices
Skills Needed: Event planning, networking, communication
Best For: Event planners, sales professionals, students
✓ Leadership experience ✓ Networking opportunities ✓ Event planning skills
📊

Research & Documentation

Conduct impact assessments, document best practices, prepare case studies, and support monitoring & evaluation activities.

Time Commitment: 1-3 months per project
Location: Mixed - field visits + remote work
Skills Needed: Research, data analysis, writing
Best For: Graduate students, researchers, analysts
✓ Research experience ✓ Publication opportunities ✓ Data skills

How to Get Started

1

Express Interest

Fill out our volunteer application form with your interests, skills, and availability. Tell us what excites you most about development work.

2

Interview & Matching

We'll have a conversation to understand your goals and match you with the right opportunity. This ensures a meaningful experience for both you and the communities.

3

Orientation & Training

Complete our volunteer orientation covering our approach, cultural sensitivity, safety protocols, and specific training for your chosen area.

4

Start Volunteering

Begin your volunteer journey with full support from our team. Regular check-ins ensure you're having a positive and impactful experience.

Volunteer Requirements & Support

What We Look For

✓ Genuine commitment to social development
✓ Respect for local cultures and communities
✓ Flexibility and adaptability
✓ Basic English communication (Hindi/Tamil helpful)
✓ Minimum age 18 (parental consent for under 21)
✓ Physical fitness for field work (if applicable)

What We Provide

🏠 Accommodation for field volunteers
🍽️ Meals during field assignments
🚗 Local transportation for work
📋 Comprehensive orientation & training
🤝 Dedicated mentor/supervisor
📜 Certificate of volunteer service
📸 Photo documentation of your work
💬 24/7 support and emergency contact
