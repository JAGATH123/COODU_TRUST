Why volunteer

Your time can transform lives

The opportunity

Volunteering with Coodu Trust offers you the opportunity to make a real, measurable difference in rural communities.

Whether you have a few hours a week or can commit to longer-term projects, there’s a meaningful way for you to contribute to sustainable development and community empowerment. You’ll join 500+ active volunteers who have already given 10,000+ hours to communities across rural Tamil Nadu.

Find the perfect way to contribute based on your skills, interests, and availability — across 25+ volunteer programs spanning field work, professional skills, education, digital communications, events and research.

How to get started

  1. Express interest Fill out our volunteer application form with your interests, skills, and availability. Tell us what excites you most about development work.
  2. Interview & matching We’ll have a conversation to understand your goals and match you with the right opportunity. This ensures a meaningful experience for both you and the communities.
  3. Orientation & training Complete our volunteer orientation covering our approach, cultural sensitivity, safety protocols, and specific training for your chosen area.
  4. Start volunteering Begin your volunteer journey with full support from our team. Regular check-ins ensure you’re having a positive and impactful experience.

Where you can help

Volunteer opportunities

Field work & community engagement

Work directly with rural communities on watershed development, organic farming, and women’s empowerment programs. Perfect for hands-on experience in development work.

Professional skills

Share your professional expertise in areas like accounting, marketing, IT, legal, or project management to strengthen our organizational capacity.

Education & training

Conduct workshops, training sessions, and educational programs for community members, farmers, and women’s groups on various development topics.

Digital & communications

Help with social media, content creation, photography, videography, website management, and digital marketing to amplify our impact.

Event management & fundraising

Organize fundraising events, awareness campaigns, and community outreach programs to support our mission and expand our reach.

Research & documentation

Conduct impact assessments, document best practices, prepare case studies, and support monitoring & evaluation activities.

Before you apply

Volunteer requirements & support

What we look for

  1. Genuine commitment to social development
  2. Respect for local cultures and communities
  3. Flexibility and adaptability
  4. Basic English communication Hindi/Tamil helpful.
  5. Minimum age 18 Parental consent for under 21.
  6. Physical fitness for field work If applicable.

What we provide

  1. Accommodation for field volunteers
  2. Meals during field assignments
  3. Local transportation for work
  4. Comprehensive orientation & training
  5. Dedicated mentor/supervisor
  6. Certificate of volunteer service
  7. Photo documentation of your work
  8. 24/7 support and emergency contact
