Women-Led Dairy FPO Development

Empowering women farmers through collective dairy enterprise

Project Period: 2021 - 2024 (Ongoing)
Funding Partner: Tamil Nadu State FPO Acceleration Corporation (TNSFAC)
Location: Karur & Dindigul Districts

Project Objectives

Our Women-Led Dairy FPO Development program focuses on creating sustainable livelihoods for women farmers through collective dairy enterprise. By organizing women into Farmer Producer Organizations, we enable them to access better markets, quality inputs, and fair prices for their milk production. The project emphasizes women's leadership in agricultural value chains and promotes economic independence through dairy farming.

2 Women-Led FPOs
500 Women Farmers
10 Milk Collection Centers
₹25L+ Annual Turnover

Project Timeline & Milestones

2021

Foundation & Mobilization

Identified potential women dairy farmers and conducted awareness programs on collective farming and FPO benefits across target villages in Karur and Dindigul.

2022

FPO Formation

Successfully formed 2 women-led dairy FPOs with 250 women farmers each, completed legal registration, and elected women board members for leadership roles.

2023

Infrastructure Development

Established 10 milk collection centers with cooling facilities, procured milk testing equipment, and initiated direct marketing arrangements with dairy processing units.

2024

Business Operations

Achieved sustainable operations with daily milk collection of 2,000+ liters, implemented quality testing protocols, and secured premium price contracts with major dairy brands.

Key Project Components

👩‍🌾

Women Leadership Development

  • Board of Directors training for women leaders
  • Financial management and accounting skills
  • Business planning and market negotiation
  • Governance and decision-making processes
🥛

Milk Collection & Processing

  • 10 milk collection centers with cooling systems
  • Quality testing equipment and protocols
  • Automated milk testing and payment systems
  • Cold chain maintenance and transportation
🏪

Market Linkages

  • Direct contracts with dairy processing companies
  • Premium price negotiations for quality milk
  • Brand partnerships and supply agreements
  • Local market development initiatives
🐄

Dairy Farm Support

  • Cattle breed improvement programs
  • Animal health care and vaccination
  • Feed optimization and nutrition guidance
  • Modern dairy farming technique training

Impact & Outcomes

Economic Impact

  • Income Increase: 40% increase in average monthly income per farmer
  • Price Premium: ₹3-5 higher per liter compared to local rates
  • Cost Reduction: 25% savings on feed through collective procurement
  • Financial Independence: Women managing their own earnings

Social Impact

  • Women Leadership: 500 women in leadership roles
  • Decision Making: Enhanced participation in household decisions
  • Community Recognition: Improved social status and respect
  • Skill Development: Technical and business skills acquired

Technical Impact

  • Quality Improvement: 95% A-grade milk quality achieved
  • Productivity: 30% increase in milk yield per animal
  • Technology Adoption: Digital payment and testing systems
  • Best Practices: Modern dairy farming techniques implemented

Success Story

"From Individual Struggle to Collective Strength"

"Before our FPO, I was selling milk to local vendors for ₹25 per liter and often faced payment delays. As the Secretary of our Women Dairy FPO, I now negotiate with processing companies and we get ₹32 per liter with daily payments. Our monthly income has increased from ₹4,000 to ₹7,500. More importantly, we women now have a voice in business decisions and are respected leaders in our community."
Lakshmi K.
Secretary, Karur Women Dairy Farmers Producer Company
Mother of two, Karur District
