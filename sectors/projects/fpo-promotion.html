Farmer Producer Organizations (FPO) Promotion

Empowering farmers through collective action and market access

Project Period: 2015 - 2024 (Ongoing)
Funding Partner: Tamil Nadu State FPO Acceleration Corporation (TNSFAC)
Location: Dindigul & Thiruvarur Districts

Project Objectives

Under the ambitious 10,000 FPO scheme, COODU Trust has been instrumental in promoting Farmer Producer Organizations to strengthen the agricultural value chain and improve farmers' livelihoods. Our approach focuses on collective farming, shared resources, and enhanced market access to increase farmers' income and reduce input costs.

4 FPOs Promoted
1,103 Farmers Enrolled
3 Major Crops
₹50L+ Equity Grants Secured

Project Timeline & Milestones

2015-2016

Foundation Phase

Conducted awareness programs on FPO formation and management for 600 farmers in Dindigul focusing on Maize, Cotton, and Vegetables cultivation.

2021-2022

FPO Establishment

Successfully promoted 4 FPOs in Dindigul and Thiruvarur districts, enrolling over 1,000 farmers and initiating the registration process.

2022-2023

Operational Phase

Supported 4 FPOs with 1,082 farmers to secure Equity Grants and commence business operations including collective procurement and marketing.

2023-2024

Expansion & Growth

Enrolled a total of 1,103 farmers across four FPOs, focusing on high-value crops like garlic, maize, and paddy with improved market linkages.

Key Project Components

📋

FPO Formation & Registration

  • Legal entity formation under Companies Act
  • Board of Directors election and training
  • Business plan development
  • Government registration and compliance
👥

Member Mobilization

  • Farmer awareness and education campaigns
  • Membership drive across target villages
  • Share capital collection from members
  • Regular member meetings and governance
💰

Financial Support

  • Government Equity Grant facilitation
  • Bank linkage for credit access
  • Working capital support
  • Financial literacy training
🛒

Market Linkages

  • Collective procurement of inputs
  • Bulk marketing of produce
  • Value addition initiatives
  • Direct buyer connections

Impact & Outcomes

Economic Impact

  • Input Cost Reduction: 15-20% savings through collective procurement
  • Price Premium: 10-15% better prices through direct marketing
  • Income Increase: Average 25% increase in farmer income
  • Credit Access: Enhanced bank linkage for member farmers

Social Impact

  • Collective Strength: 1,103 farmers united under 4 FPOs
  • Knowledge Sharing: Peer-to-peer learning among members
  • Leadership Development: Farmer leaders as directors
  • Community Empowerment: Strengthened rural institutions

Agricultural Impact

  • Crop Diversification: Focus on high-value crops like garlic
  • Quality Improvement: Better input quality and practices
  • Technology Adoption: Modern farming techniques
  • Sustainable Practices: Reduced chemical dependency

Success Story

"Our FPO has transformed how we do farming"

"Before joining the FPO, I used to buy seeds and fertilizers individually at high prices and sell my produce to local traders at whatever price they offered. Now, through our FPO, we buy inputs collectively at 20% lower cost and sell our garlic directly to processing companies at premium prices. My annual income has increased from ₹80,000 to ₹1,20,000. More importantly, we have a voice in the market now."
Murugan K.
Board Member, Dindigul Garlic Farmers Producer Company
Farmer from Natham Block, Dindigul
