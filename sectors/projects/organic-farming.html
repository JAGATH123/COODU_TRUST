Organic Farming Promotion

Chemical-free agriculture for healthier food and sustainable farming

Project Period: 2013 - 2024 (Ongoing)
Funding Partner: Ministry of Agriculture & State Agriculture Department
Location: Dindigul, Karur, Thiruvarur, Madurai & Theni Districts

Project Objectives

Our Organic Farming Promotion program aims to transition farmers from chemical-intensive agriculture to sustainable, organic farming practices. We provide comprehensive training on natural farming methods, organic certification processes, and market linkages for premium organic produce. The program focuses on soil health restoration, biodiversity conservation, and farmer health while ensuring economic viability through reduced input costs and premium market access.

3,700+ Farmers Trained
15,000 Acres Converted
80% Chemical Reduction
1,200 Certified Farmers

Project Timeline & Milestones

2013-2015

Awareness & Foundation

Conducted awareness programs on organic farming benefits, established demonstration plots, and trained 800 farmers in basic organic practices and soil health management.

2016-2018

Scale-up & Certification

Expanded to 5 districts, trained 1,500 additional farmers, initiated organic certification processes, and established organic input production centers for bio-fertilizers and pesticides.

2019-2021

Market Linkage Development

Facilitated organic certification for 800 farmers, established direct market linkages with organic buyers, and created farmer producer groups for collective marketing.

2022-2024

Sustainability & Innovation

Implemented digital traceability systems, expanded to 3,700+ farmers, achieved 1,200 certified organic farmers, and established sustainable supply chains with premium brands.

Key Project Components

🌱

Organic Practices Training

  • Natural fertilizer and pesticide preparation
  • Composting and vermicomposting techniques
  • Crop rotation and intercropping systems
  • Soil health restoration methods
📜

Certification Support

  • Organic certification process guidance
  • Documentation and record maintenance
  • Inspection support and compliance
  • Group certification facilitation
🏪

Market Development

  • Direct buyer-farmer connections
  • Premium price negotiations
  • Organic retail partnerships
  • Export market facilitation
🧪

Input Production

  • Bio-fertilizer production units
  • Organic pesticide preparation
  • Quality testing and standardization
  • Cost-effective input supply chains

Impact & Outcomes

Environmental Impact

  • Chemical Reduction: 80% decrease in synthetic pesticide usage
  • Soil Health: 25% increase in organic matter content
  • Water Quality: Reduced groundwater contamination
  • Biodiversity: 40% increase in beneficial insects

Economic Impact

  • Premium Pricing: 25-40% higher prices for organic produce
  • Input Cost Savings: 35% reduction in cultivation costs
  • Certified Farmers: ₹25,000 additional annual income
  • Market Access: Direct contracts with 50+ buyers

Health & Social Impact

  • Farmer Health: Reduced chemical exposure incidents
  • Consumer Health: Chemical-free food production
  • Knowledge Transfer: Peer learning networks established
  • Community Pride: Recognition as organic farming leaders

Success Story

"From Chemicals to Certified Organic Success"

"I used to spend ₹40,000 per season on chemicals for my 5-acre farm and was getting health problems. After the organic farming training, I completely stopped chemicals 4 years ago. Now I save ₹35,000 on inputs, my soil is healthier, and I get ₹45 per kg for organic tomatoes compared to ₹25 for regular ones. I'm certified organic and supply to premium stores in Chennai. My family's health has improved dramatically."
Murugan S.
Certified Organic Farmer
Theni District
