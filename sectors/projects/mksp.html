Mahila Kisan Sashaktikaran Program (MKSP)

Empowering women farmers through sustainable agriculture and organic farming

Project Period: 2019 - 2021
Funding Partner: Government of Tamil Nadu - Department of Agriculture
Location: Dindigul, Karur & Thiruvarur Districts

Project Objectives

The Mahila Kisan Sashaktikaran Program (MKSP) was designed to empower women farmers by enhancing their participation in agriculture through sustainable farming practices. Our comprehensive approach included training on organic farming techniques, vermicomposting, integrated pest management, and value addition. The program aimed to reduce input costs, improve soil health, and increase farm income while promoting women's leadership in agriculture.

5,000 Women Trained
200 Villages Covered
40% Cost Reduction
2,500 Vermicompost Units

Project Timeline & Milestones

2019

Program Launch & Mobilization

Conducted baseline surveys, identified target villages, and mobilized 5,000 women farmers across three districts for comprehensive organic farming training programs.

2020

Training & Capacity Building

Implemented extensive training modules on organic farming, vermicomposting, IPM techniques, and established 2,500 individual vermicompost units at household level.

2021

Implementation & Monitoring

Supported farmers in adopting organic practices, monitored crop performance, facilitated organic certification processes, and documented success stories and best practices.

Key Project Components

🌱

Organic Farming Training

  • Soil health management and testing
  • Organic seed production techniques
  • Natural fertilizer preparation methods
  • Crop rotation and companion planting
🪱

Vermicomposting Units

  • Individual household vermicompost setups
  • Earthworm breeding and maintenance
  • Quality vermicompost production
  • Marketing surplus vermicompost
🐛

Integrated Pest Management

  • Natural pest control methods
  • Beneficial insect conservation
  • Organic pesticide preparation
  • Disease prevention strategies
📜

Organic Certification

  • Organic certification process guidance
  • Documentation and record keeping
  • Premium market linkage facilitation
  • Quality assurance protocols

Impact & Outcomes

Economic Impact

  • Input Cost Reduction: 40% decrease in farming input costs
  • Premium Pricing: 20-30% higher prices for organic produce
  • Additional Income: ₹15,000 annual income from vermicompost
  • Soil Health: Improved fertility reducing long-term costs

Environmental Impact

  • Chemical Reduction: 80% reduction in chemical pesticide use
  • Soil Health: Improved organic matter and microbial activity
  • Water Conservation: Better water retention in organic soils
  • Biodiversity: Enhanced beneficial insect populations

Social Impact

  • Women Empowerment: 5,000 women trained as organic experts
  • Health Benefits: Reduced exposure to harmful chemicals
  • Knowledge Transfer: Peer-to-peer learning networks established
  • Community Leadership: Women leading organic farming groups

Success Story

"From Chemicals to Organic Gold"

"MKSP completely changed my farming approach. I was spending ₹25,000 per season on chemicals and fertilizers. After the training, I started using vermicompost and organic methods. Now my input costs are only ₹10,000, my soil is healthier, and I get premium prices for organic vegetables. The vermicompost unit gives me an additional ₹1,200 per month. My family's health has improved too, as we eat chemical-free food."
Kamala S.
Organic Vegetable Farmer
Dindigul District
