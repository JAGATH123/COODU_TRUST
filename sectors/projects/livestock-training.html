Livestock & Dairy Training

Modern dairy farming techniques and livestock management

Project Period: 2013 - 2024 (Ongoing)
Funding Partner: Tamil Nadu Animal Husbandry Department & Private Partnerships
Location: Dindigul, Karur, Thiruvarur & Surrounding Districts

Project Objectives

Our Livestock & Dairy Training program focuses on enhancing the productivity and profitability of dairy farming through modern techniques and best practices. We provide comprehensive training on cattle management, feed optimization, disease prevention, artificial insemination, and milk quality improvement. The program aims to transform traditional dairy farming into a scientific and profitable enterprise while ensuring animal welfare and sustainable practices.

6,400+ Farmers Trained
15,000+ Cattle Benefited
35% Milk Yield Increase
50+ Villages Covered

Project Timeline & Milestones

2013-2015

Foundation Phase

Established dairy training centers, developed curriculum with veterinary experts, and conducted baseline assessments of traditional dairy practices in target villages.

2016-2018

Expansion & Technology Integration

Introduced artificial insemination services, feed optimization techniques, and disease prevention protocols. Trained 2,500 farmers in modern dairy management practices.

2019-2021

Quality Enhancement

Focused on milk quality improvement, hygiene practices, and value addition. Established mobile veterinary services and emergency healthcare support for livestock.

2022-2024

Sustainable Systems

Implemented digital record keeping, integrated IoT solutions for cattle monitoring, and established sustainable feed production systems with improved nutritional outcomes.

Impact & Outcomes

Productivity Impact

  • Milk Yield: 35% average increase in daily milk production
  • Calving Interval: Reduced from 18 to 14 months
  • Feed Efficiency: 25% improvement in feed conversion ratio
  • Lactation Period: Extended by 45-60 days on average

Economic Impact

  • Income Increase: ₹18,000 additional annual income per farmer
  • Cost Reduction: 20% decrease in veterinary expenses
  • Quality Premium: ₹2-3 higher per liter for quality milk
  • Asset Value: 30% increase in cattle market value

Technical Impact

  • Modern Practices: 85% adoption of scientific methods
  • Disease Reduction: 60% decrease in cattle mortality
  • Milk Quality: 90% farmers achieving A-grade standards
  • Record Keeping: Digital monitoring systems implemented

Success Story

"From 8 Liters to 20 Liters Per Day"

"Before the training, my two local cows gave only 8 liters of milk per day. After learning about proper feeding, cattle care, and artificial insemination, I now have crossbred cows producing 20 liters daily. The veterinary support helped reduce cattle diseases, and my monthly income from dairy has increased from ₹6,000 to ₹15,000. The scientific approach has made dairy farming profitable for my family."
Raman K.
Progressive Dairy Farmer
Natham Block, Dindigul District
