Community Development & Governance

Strengthening local institutions and democratic participation

Building Strong Communities

Community development lies at the heart of sustainable change. We work to strengthen local institutions, enhance democratic participation, and build community resilience. Our approach includes strengthening Panchayati Raj institutions, organizing farmer collectives, and providing immediate relief during disasters while building long-term community capacity for self-governance and development.
