Health & Sanitation

Ensuring healthy communities through preventive healthcare and sanitation

Building Healthy Communities

Health is fundamental to human development and community well-being. Our health and sanitation programs focus on preventive healthcare, improved sanitation facilities, and health awareness. Through partnerships with government agencies like TANSACS and comprehensive community-based initiatives, we work to ensure that everyone has access to basic healthcare and sanitation facilities.

15,000+ People Reached
2,500+ Toilets Built
8,000+ Health Checkups
95% Immunization Rate
