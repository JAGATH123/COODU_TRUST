Microfinance & Financial Inclusion

Providing financial access and credit services for women's economic empowerment

Understanding Microfinance & Financial Inclusion

Microfinance and financial inclusion programs are designed to provide financial services to low-income individuals and communities who typically lack access to traditional banking and financial services. These programs focus on offering small loans, savings opportunities, insurance, and other financial products to help people start or expand small businesses, improve their livelihoods, and build financial resilience.

Our Microfinance & Financial Inclusion initiatives specifically target women's Self-Help Groups (SHGs) to promote entrepreneurship, poverty alleviation, and economic empowerment. By providing micro-credit and financial literacy, we enable women to become financially independent, start income-generating activities, and contribute to their household and community economic development.

Key Components of Microfinance & Financial Inclusion:

  • Micro-credit Services: Providing small loans to women's SHGs for income-generating activities and entrepreneurship development
  • Savings Mobilization: Encouraging and facilitating regular savings habits among women through group savings schemes
  • Financial Literacy: Building financial knowledge and skills for better money management and business planning
  • Poverty Alleviation: Targeting the poorest women to break the cycle of poverty through financial access
  • Entrepreneurship Promotion: Supporting women entrepreneurs with credit, training, and market linkages

Our Microfinance & Financial Inclusion Projects (2002-2024)

Microfinance and Poverty Alleviation

2002-2003
Core Foundation Program

The project's core function was providing microfinance specifically to women's Self-Help Groups (SHGs) to promote entrepreneurship and poverty alleviation. This foundational program established the framework for delivering financial services to rural women, enabling them to access credit for income-generating activities and small business development.

Focus Areas: Microfinance, SHGs, Rural Women, Women's Empowerment, Poverty Alleviation, Entrepreneurship Development

Rashtriya Mahila Kosh (RMK) Project

2009-2012
National Scheme Program

A comprehensive micro-credit program exclusively designed for poor women, providing loans for income-generating activities through the Rashtriya Mahila Kosh framework. This national scheme focused on financial inclusion of the most vulnerable women, offering them access to formal credit systems and supporting their journey towards economic independence.

Focus Areas: Rashtriya Mahila Kosh (RMK), Micro-credit, Poor Women, SHGs, Income Generation, Financial Inclusion

Microfinance & Financial Inclusion Impact

💰

Financial Access

Provided microfinance services to over 3,500 women through 450+ SHGs, enabling access to formal financial services and credit facilities for the first time.

🏪

Entrepreneurship Development

Supported hundreds of women entrepreneurs to start and expand their businesses, creating sustainable livelihood opportunities and local economic growth.

📉

Poverty Reduction

Significantly contributed to poverty alleviation by providing targeted financial support to the poorest women, helping them break the cycle of poverty.

🏦

Financial Literacy

Enhanced financial knowledge and skills among rural women, enabling better money management, savings habits, and informed financial decision-making.

🤝

Group Solidarity

Strengthened women's collectives through SHG-based microfinance, creating peer support systems and collective responsibility for financial management.

📈

Economic Independence

Achieved high loan repayment rates (95%) demonstrating women's financial responsibility and successful transition to economic independence.
