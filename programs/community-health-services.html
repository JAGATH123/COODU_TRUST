Understanding

What community health services mean

What are community health services?

Community health services deliver essential healthcare directly to communities — particularly in underserved and remote areas where access to healthcare facilities is limited — bringing medical care closer to the people who need it most.

Our initiatives provide preventive and curative healthcare through mobile medical camps, emergency response services and community-based facilities. Coodu Trust has served 15,000+ people across 50+ villages through 200+ health camps staffed by qualified doctors and medical personnel.

The portfolio spans general health camps with medical teams, essential emergency support such as an affordable mortuary van service, and specialised care centres for specific health emergencies and epidemic response. We work with qualified medical professionals, paramedics and community health workers to keep comprehensive coverage and 24/7 emergency response in place for rural and marginalised communities.

  • Mobile health camps

How we deliver community health

  1. Community outreach & awareness Health education, awareness programmes and door-to-door services delivered through community health workers.
  2. Mobile health camps Regular medical camps with qualified doctors and medical personnel providing health check-ups and basic treatment.
  3. Emergency & support services Affordable mortuary van services and 24/7 emergency response for poor and disadvantaged communities.
  4. Specialised care Dedicated facilities for specific health emergencies and epidemic response, such as the COVID Care Centre.

Why this work matters

The challenge on the ground — and our answer

The challenge

Clinics out of reach

In remote and underserved villages, the nearest healthcare facility is often too far — so check-ups are skipped and illness goes untreated.

Our response

Take the doctor to the village

200+ mobile health camps with qualified doctors and medical teams have brought check-ups and treatment to 50+ villages — serving 15,000+ people.

The challenge

Essential services the poor can't afford

For poor and disadvantaged families, essential health system support — including dignified mortuary services — can be priced out of reach.

Our response

Affordable emergency support

Under the Tamil Nadu Health System Project we run an affordable mortuary van service, backed by 24/7 emergency support for the communities that need it most.

The challenge

A pandemic with nowhere to isolate

When COVID-19 struck, rural families had little access to isolation facilities, critical care or reliable information.

Our response

A dedicated COVID response

Our COVID Care Centre (2020–2022) provided critical care, isolation facilities and treatment, while relief works delivered prevention, awareness and essential supplies.

The challenge

Illness caught too late

Without preventive care and health education, conditions go undetected until they become emergencies.

Our response

Prevention before cure

Health education, awareness programmes and door-to-door outreach by community health workers put preventive healthcare within everyone's reach.
