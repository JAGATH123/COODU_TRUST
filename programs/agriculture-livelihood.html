Agriculture & Livelihood Enhancement

Improving farm-based income and strengthening the rural economy

Transforming Rural Livelihoods

Agriculture remains the backbone of rural Tamil Nadu, supporting millions of livelihoods. Our Agriculture & Livelihood Enhancement programs focus on improving farm-based income, promoting sustainable agriculture practices, and strengthening the allied rural economy. Through Farmer Producer Organizations, livestock development, and sustainable farming techniques, we empower farmers to achieve food security and economic stability.

Success Stories

Women-Led Dairy FPO Transforms Lives

"The dairy FPO has changed everything for us. We now get better prices for our milk, have access to quality feed, and most importantly, we have a voice in the market. Our monthly income has doubled from ₹3,000 to ₹6,000."

Meera Devi FPO Secretary, Karur

Organic Farming Reduces Costs, Increases Yield

"After the MKSP training, I switched to organic farming. My input costs dropped by 40%, soil health improved, and I'm getting premium prices for organic produce. The vermicompost unit is an additional income source."

Kamala S. Organic Farmer, Dindigul
