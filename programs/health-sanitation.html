Health, sanitation & waste management

Ensuring healthy communities through comprehensive health interventions and sanitation infrastructure.

25,000+ People reached
3,500+ Sanitation facilities
15,000+ Health screenings
95% Disease prevention rate

Our health mission

Building healthier communities

How we deliver

We work closely with government health departments and community organizations to ensure sustainable and effective health outcomes.

Health and sanitation programs are fundamental to community development and quality of life. Our comprehensive health initiatives focus on disease prevention, water quality management and sanitation infrastructure development — reaching 25,000+ people and delivering 15,000+ health screenings across rural and marginalized communities.

The sector encompasses targeted disease interventions, water quality testing and safety measures, and the development of sanitation infrastructure including toilets, drainage systems and waste management facilities. We have built 3,500+ sanitation facilities, achieved 90% sanitation coverage in target communities, and sustained a 95% disease prevention rate across program areas.

What this sector delivers

  1. Disease prevention Targeted interventions that reduced disease incidence by 75% in program areas.
  2. Clean water access Safe drinking water for 25,000+ people through quality testing, purification systems and infrastructure.
  3. Sanitation coverage 3,500+ toilets and waste management facilities built, achieving 90% coverage in target communities.
  4. Healthcare access 15,000+ health screenings and checkups for early detection and treatment.
