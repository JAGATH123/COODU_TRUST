Strategic Planning & Advisory Services

Expert guidance for development programs, organizational growth, and impact maximization

Understanding Strategic Planning & Advisory Services

Strategic Planning & Advisory Services provide expert guidance to NGOs, government departments, and development organizations in designing, implementing, and evaluating high-impact programs. With 25 years of on-ground experience across six development sectors, Coodu Trust offers comprehensive consultancy services that transform program ideas into measurable results.

Our advisory services draw from proven expertise in watershed management (640 micro-watersheds, 24 districts), solid waste management (₹7.1 crore Dindigul MC contract), skill development (18,592+ youth trained), and women empowerment (2,168+ beneficiaries). We combine sectoral knowledge with strategic thinking to help organizations maximize impact, improve efficiency, and achieve sustainability.

Key Components of Our Strategic Advisory Services:

  • Program Design & Development: Comprehensive planning, logical frameworks, and intervention strategies for new development programs
  • Implementation Support: On-ground guidance for program execution, stakeholder coordination, and operational management
  • Monitoring & Evaluation: Impact assessment frameworks, data collection systems, and performance tracking mechanisms
  • Organizational Development: Institutional strengthening, governance improvement, and capacity building for NGOs
  • Grant Proposal Development: Funding proposals, project reports, and donor communication strategies
  • Partnership Facilitation: Multi-stakeholder coordination, CSR partnerships, and government collaboration frameworks

Our Consultancy Approach

Coodu Trust follows a systematic methodology combining evidence-based planning with practical implementation experience.

🔍

Comprehensive Needs Assessment

In-depth analysis of context, stakeholders, challenges, and opportunities. We conduct field visits, stakeholder consultations, and data review to understand the ground reality before designing interventions.

📋

Evidence-Based Program Design

Development of logical frameworks, theory of change models, and intervention strategies grounded in best practices. Clear objectives, activities, timelines, and budget planning ensure feasibility.

🛤️

Implementation Roadmap

Phased execution plans with clear milestones, resource allocation, and risk mitigation strategies. Includes staffing plans, procurement schedules, and community mobilization protocols.

📊

Monitoring & Evaluation Frameworks

Development of indicators, data collection tools, and reporting systems for tracking progress and measuring impact. Regular reviews enable course correction and continuous improvement.

👥

Institutional Capacity Building

Training programs for staff, board members, and community partners. Strengthening organizational systems, governance structures, and operational processes for sustainability.

📚

Knowledge Transfer & Documentation

Creation of best practice documentation, training manuals, and case studies. Ensuring long-term sustainability through knowledge institutionalization and local capacity development.

Our Advisory Service Areas

NGO & Community Organization Development

Institutional Strengthening

Comprehensive consultancy for NGOs and community-based organizations on governance, strategic planning, financial management, and program excellence. We support grassroots organizations in becoming professionally managed, sustainable institutions.

Key Services:

  • Organizational assessments and strategic planning
  • Board development and governance strengthening
  • Financial systems and compliance frameworks

Government Program Implementation Support

Field Agency Services

Support to government departments in implementing rural development, health, sanitation, and skill development programs. Coodu Trust is empanelled with Ministry of Jal Shakti and has managed programs worth ₹7.1+ crore.

Key Services:

  • Field implementation of central/state schemes
  • Community mobilization and participation
  • Monitoring, reporting, and compliance management
  • Stakeholder coordination and convergence
  • Impact assessment and documentation

Corporate CSR Program Design

CSR Partnerships

End-to-end CSR program development for corporates including need assessment, program design, implementation, and impact reporting. Current partners include Renault Nissan, ATC Tires, United Way Mumbai, and SBI Foundation.

Key Services:

  • CSR strategy development and compliance

  • Program implementation and management
  • Impact measurement and reporting for Companies Act 2013
