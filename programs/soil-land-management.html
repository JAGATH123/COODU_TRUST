Soil & Land Management

Soil health improvement, erosion control, and sustainable land use practices

Understanding Soil & Land Management

Soil and Land Management encompasses the sustainable use, conservation, and improvement of soil resources to maintain and enhance agricultural productivity while protecting environmental quality. It involves implementing practices that prevent soil degradation, improve soil health, and optimize land use for long-term sustainability.

Our soil and land management programs focus on promoting sustainable agriculture practices, organic farming techniques, and indigenous knowledge systems that enhance soil fertility and maintain ecological balance. We emphasize training farmers in sustainable land management techniques that improve soil health while reducing dependency on chemical inputs.

Key Components of Soil & Land Management:

  • Sustainable Agriculture: Promoting eco-friendly farming practices that maintain soil health and productivity
  • Organic Farming: Training farmers in chemical-free agriculture using natural inputs and biological pest control
  • Indigenous Practices: Reviving traditional farming knowledge and techniques for sustainable land management
  • Soil Health Improvement: Implementing practices to enhance soil fertility, structure, and biological activity
  • Vermi-composting: Producing organic fertilizers through earthworm composting for soil enrichment
  • Erosion Control: Preventing soil loss through appropriate land use and conservation practices
