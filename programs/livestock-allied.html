Livestock & Allied Activities

Promoting livestock development and allied activities for diversified income sources

Understanding Livestock & Allied Activities

Livestock and Allied Activities encompass the breeding, rearing, and management of domesticated animals for agricultural purposes, including cattle, buffalo, goats, sheep, poultry, and other farm animals. This sector also includes allied activities such as dairy farming, animal husbandry, veterinary services, feed production, and value-added processing of animal products. Livestock plays a crucial role in rural economies, providing regular income, employment opportunities, and nutritional security to farming families.

Our Livestock and Allied Activities programs focus on improving animal productivity, enhancing veterinary care, establishing dairy enterprises, and developing market linkages for livestock products. We emphasize sustainable livestock management practices that ensure animal welfare, environmental protection, and economic viability. Our integrated approach includes providing quality breeding stock, training in modern animal husbandry techniques, establishing feed production units, and creating value chains for milk, eggs, and meat products.

Key Components of Livestock & Allied Activities:

  • Dairy Development: Establishing milk production systems, processing units, and marketing networks for sustainable dairy enterprises
  • Animal Husbandry Training: Providing technical knowledge in breeding, feeding, healthcare, and management of livestock
  • Veterinary Services: Ensuring animal health through preventive care, treatment facilities, and vaccination programs
  • Feed and Fodder Development: Cultivating nutritious fodder crops and establishing feed production systems
  • Goshala Management: Operating cattle care centers focused on animal welfare, milk production, and breed improvement
  • Value Addition: Processing milk and other animal products to increase market value and farmer incomes

Benefits of Livestock & Allied Activities

💰 Regular Income

Provides steady cash flow through daily milk sales, egg production, and periodic sale of animals.

🍼 Nutritional Security

Ensures household access to high-quality protein through milk, eggs, and meat products.

♻️ Resource Utilization

Converts agricultural by-products and crop residues into valuable animal products through efficient feed conversion.

🌱 Organic Fertilizer

Provides high-quality manure for crop production, improving soil fertility and reducing chemical fertilizer dependency.

👩 Women's Empowerment

Creates income opportunities for rural women through dairy entrepreneurship and livestock management activities.

🛡️ Risk Diversification

Reduces agricultural risk by providing alternative income sources that complement crop farming activities.
