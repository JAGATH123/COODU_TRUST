Livestock & Allied Activities

Promoting livestock development and allied activities for diversified income sources

Understanding Livestock & Allied Activities

What is Livestock & Allied Activities? 🐄

Livestock & Allied Activities encompass the breeding, rearing, and management of domesticated animals including dairy farming, animal husbandry, veterinary services, and value-added processing.

Our approach focuses on improving animal productivity, enhancing veterinary care, establishing dairy enterprises, and developing market linkages for livestock products through sustainable management practices that ensure animal welfare and economic viability.

Our integrated approach includes quality breeding stock, modern animal husbandry techniques, feed production units, and value chains for milk, eggs, and meat products to provide regular income and nutritional security.

Key Components

🥛 Dairy Development
🩺 Veterinary Services
🌾 Feed & Fodder
🏭 Value Addition

Our Livestock Development Process

1

Animal Selection

Identifying quality breeding stock and livestock varieties suitable for local conditions.

2

Husbandry Training

Training farmers in modern animal care, feeding, breeding, and disease prevention.

3

Health Management

Providing veterinary services, vaccination programs, and health monitoring systems.

4

Market Integration

Establishing processing units and market linkages for livestock products.

Livestock & Allied Activities Impact

🐄

Livestock Development

Improved animal productivity through better breeding, feeding, and healthcare practices, enhancing milk production and farmer incomes.

🥛

Dairy Enterprise

Established sustainable dairy businesses through entrepreneurship training, milk collection systems, and market linkage development.

🎓

Skill Enhancement

Animal Welfare

Promoted animal welfare through goshala management, veterinary care, and humane treatment practices.

Benefits of Livestock & Allied Activities

💰 Regular Income

Provides steady cash flow through daily milk sales, egg production, and periodic sale of animals.

🍼 Nutritional Security

Ensures household access to high-quality protein through milk, eggs, and meat products.

♻️ Resource Utilization

Converts agricultural by-products and crop residues into valuable animal products through efficient feed conversion.

🌱 Organic Fertilizer

Provides high-quality manure for crop production, improving soil fertility and reducing chemical fertilizer dependency.

👩 Women's Empowerment

Creates income opportunities for rural women through dairy entrepreneurship and livestock management activities.

🛡️ Risk Diversification

Reduces agricultural risk by providing alternative income sources that complement crop farming activities.
