IWMP Overview

The Integrated Watershed Management Programme (IWMP) is a flagship initiative of the Department of Land Resources, Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India. The program is designed to restore the ecological balance of degraded landscapes by treating watersheds in a holistic and integrated manner.

🎯 Primary Objective

Harness, conserve, and develop natural resources like soil, water, and vegetative cover to prevent soil erosion, regenerate vegetation, harvest rainwater, and recharge the groundwater table.

Coodu Trust serves as a key implementing partner for the IWMP, working in close coordination with the District Watershed Development Agency (DWDA) across numerous districts in Tamil Nadu. The program's strategy is centered on effective community participation, ensuring that development plans are based on local needs and land capabilities.

By strengthening community-based institutions and promoting sustainable agro-based activities, the IWMP aims to enhance the income of individuals and build long-term environmental and economic resilience.

Key Features & Achievements

🗺️

Extensive Geographical Coverage

570 Watersheds Managed

As of 2017-18, Coodu Trust's IWMP interventions spanned 570 watersheds across 14 districts in Tamil Nadu, including Karur, Tirunelveli, Pudukkottai, Virudhunagar, Krishnagiri, Sivagangai, Tiruvallur, Dharmapuri, and Salem, among others.

👥

Significant Community Impact

1.7L
Households
8.5L
Population

The program has reached approximately 1,70,000 households, covering a population of over 8,50,000 people and treating a total area of 33,500 hectares across Tamil Nadu.

🎯

Holistic Implementation

Key components of the program include:

  • • Soil & Water Conservation: Implementing measures to prevent soil run-off and conserve moisture
  • • Water Resource Management: Focusing on rainwater harvesting and recharging the groundwater table
  • • Livelihood Promotion: Enabling multi-cropping and introducing diverse, sustainable agro-based activities
  • • Capacity Building: Conducting extensive training and exposure visits for community members
📈

Progress in 2022–2024

🎯 Focus Districts

Pudukkottai, Ramanathapuram, Virudhunagar, Perambalur, Dharmapuri, Krishnagiri, and Thoothukudi

📚 Capacity Building Focus

Coodu Trust acts as coordinating agency for training and exposure visits to agricultural institutions like TNAU and KVK Gandhigram, focusing on knowledge transfer and skill development.
