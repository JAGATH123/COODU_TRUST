Returning to Nature During Drought Crisis (2002-2003)

Program Overview

The Sustainable Agriculture Training program was a crucial initiative implemented by Coodu Trust during 2002–2003, a period marked by significant drought in Tamil Nadu. The program was developed in response to the twin challenges of water scarcity and widespread degradation of farmland that had become "fully chemicalised" from overuse of synthetic inputs.

⚠️ Crisis Context

The training was specifically targeted at farming communities in districts officially declared as drought-prone by the government, addressing urgent needs during a severe water crisis.

The primary purpose was to reintroduce and promote sustainable agricultural concepts that improve soil fertility and enhance productivity naturally, thereby reducing farmers' dependency on chemical fertilizers and improving the long-term health of their land.

By conducting workshops and seminars, the program aimed to build positive links between economic activity and environmental stewardship, with a focus on "returning to Nature" and preserving the region's natural capital for future generations.
