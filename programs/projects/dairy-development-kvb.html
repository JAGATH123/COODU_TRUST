🥛 WOMEN-LED DAIRY ENTERPRISE

Dairy Development Programme

Women's Agri-Entrepreneurship through Dairy Farming — Supported by Karur Vysya Bank (2022-Present)

🎯 Program Mission

Supporting the economic empowerment of women farmers through collective action in dairy farming, by establishing a women-led Farmer Producer Company, creating milk collection infrastructure, and providing comprehensive training in dairy management, fodder cultivation, and para-veterinary services — transforming women from subsistence farmers into agri-entrepreneurs.

Programme Overview

Coodu Trust, with the support of Karur Vysya Bank (KVB), has successfully promoted a Farmer Producer Company in Tharangampatti, Karur District, under the name Tharangampatti Dairy Farmers Producer Company Limited (TDPCL). This initiative is specifically designed to empower women farmers and encourage them to become agri-entrepreneurs, with a focus on improving their dairy farming practices and creating sustainable income opportunities.

🎯 Core Objectives

Support the economic empowerment of women farmers through collective action, while improving the productivity and profitability of dairy farming in the region through scientific management practices, infrastructure development, and market linkages that ensure fair prices and weekly payments to women producers.

The programme originated under the State Balanced Growth Fund (SBGF), a unique programme by the State Planning Commission to address regional imbalances in per capita income, employment, poverty, health, education, and gender issues. The State Planning Commission identified Coodu Trust as the coordinating agency to implement the "Sustainable Livelihood Enhancement through Scientific Management of Dairy Animals" project at Kadavur Block, Karur District.

A total of 500 women farmers have been enrolled as members of TDPCL, and 100 Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) have been formed. Ten milk collection centres have been established, processing 1,500 to 1,800 litres of milk daily. The milk is marketed through Raj Dairy, with women earning between Rs. 32 to Rs. 45 per litre based on the SNF (Solids-Not-Fat) value, with weekly payments ensuring regular income flow. Karur Vysya Bank provided a dedicated milk transport vehicle, and an application has been made to SBGF for a loan to acquire 500 milch animals with subsidy.

Key Activities & Achievements

🏢

Tharangampatti Dairy FPC Formation

500 Women Farmers | 100 JLGs

Tharangampatti Dairy Farmers Producer Company Limited (TDPCL) was promoted with 500 women farmers as members, providing them a platform to collectively manage and market their milk production. 100 Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) were formed to organise the women into manageable operational units for dairy activities.

🥛

Milk Collection Infrastructure

10 Centres | 1,500-1,800 Litres/Day

Ten milk collection centres were established across Kadavur Block, making it easier for women farmers to deliver their milk efficiently and receive fair prices. The collection unit processes 1,500 to 1,800 litres of milk daily, ensuring consistent and large-scale procurement with a steady market for all participating women farmers.

💰

Marketing & Income Generation

Earnings Structure:

  • • Marketing Partner: Raj Dairy
  • • Price Range: ₹32 to ₹45 per litre (based on SNF value)
  • • Payment Cycle: Weekly payments to all beneficiaries
  • • Transport: Dedicated milk vehicle funded by Karur Vysya Bank

Milk is marketed through Raj Dairy with prices determined by SNF (Solids-Not-Fat) content, ensuring quality-based fair pricing. Weekly payments provide women with regular, predictable income, significantly improving their economic stability and household financial management.

🎓

Dairy Farming Training

260 Women Trained (2022-23) | 100 Women in Fodder & Azolla (2023-24)

Training Modules:

  • • Fodder cultivation techniques for year-round nutritious livestock feed
  • • Livestock management for improved health and milk yield
  • • Animal nutrition and health management including vaccination schedules
  • • Disease prevention and feeding practices
  • • Azolla cultivation — high-protein aquatic plant as natural feed supplement

Comprehensive training on dairy farming was delivered to women farmers, covering scientific methods of livestock rearing, feeding schedules, and health management to maximise milk yield and cow health.

👨‍⚕️

Para-Veterinary Youth Training

20 Youth | 30-Day Programme

A specialised 30-day training programme was conducted for 20 youth from the area on Para-Veterinary Services, with support from the Veterinary Department. This equipped local youth with skills to provide veterinary care to dairy animals, improving livestock health and productivity in the community while creating employment opportunities for rural youth.

🚛

Infrastructure & Scale-Up

Infrastructure Developed:

  • • 10 milk collection centres operational
  • • Dedicated milk transport vehicle (funded by KVB)
  • • SBGF loan application for 500 milch animals with subsidy
  • • Quality-based SNF testing at collection centres

Karur Vysya Bank supported the programme by funding a dedicated vehicle for transporting milk from collection centres to the marketing partner, ensuring cold-chain efficiency. An application to SBGF for a subsidised loan to acquire 500 milch animals will further expand the programme's scale.

Programme Impact

Economic Empowerment

Women have transitioned from subsistence farming to agri-entrepreneurship. The added income from dairy farming — averaging ₹34 per litre with weekly payments — has significantly improved economic status and financial independence of women farmers and their families.

Improved Dairy Productivity

Training in fodder cultivation, Azolla farming, and veterinary care has led to healthier cows, better milk yield, and increased overall productivity. Milk yield has shown remarkable improvement as a result of better feeding techniques and management practices.

Community & Market Development

TDPCL has created a strong support network for women farmers through milk collection centres and collective marketing via Raj Dairy. This network ensures better prices and a steady market, making women less vulnerable to market fluctuations.

Youth Employment

The 30-day para-veterinary training for 20 youth has created local employment opportunities, ensuring that veterinary care is accessible within the community and not dependent on external services.
