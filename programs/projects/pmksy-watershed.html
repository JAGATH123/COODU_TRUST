Program Overview

The Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) is a centrally sponsored scheme implemented by Coodu Trust in collaboration with the District Watershed Development Agency. The program's primary objective is to improve the productive potential of rainfed and degraded lands through an integrated watershed management approach.

🎯 Core Objectives

Strengthening community-based institutions, promoting sustainable livelihoods, and ensuring long-term agricultural sustainability for farmers in project areas through comprehensive capacity building.

During the 2022–2024 period, the program's implementation was focused on extensive capacity-building initiatives. The geographical scope covered seven districts, including Dindigul, Ramanathapuram, Thoothukudi, Virudhunagar, Perambalur, Krishnagiri, and Dharmapuri.

The target beneficiaries were a diverse group of local stakeholders, including members of watershed committees, farmers, and Self-Help Group (SHG) members, who are crucial for the sustainable management of local resources.

Key Achievements & Activities (2022–2024)

🎓

Total Beneficiaries Trained

2,408 Community Members Trained

A total of 2,408 individuals received comprehensive training, equipping them with modern agricultural techniques and sustainable practices for enhanced productivity and environmental stewardship.

🎯

Capacity Building Programs

The core of the program involved focused block-level training sessions on critical topics:

  • • Roles and Responsibilities of Watershed Committees for effective governance
  • • Advanced Soil and Water Conservation Techniques
  • • Skill Development for establishing agriculture-related Micro-Enterprises
  • • Sustainable practices like Vermicomposting and Organic Farming
  • • Water conservation through Micro-Irrigation Techniques for field crops
🏢

Exposure Visits

Organized training-cum-exposure visits to renowned agricultural institutions to provide participants with practical knowledge of modern cropping and water management practices.
