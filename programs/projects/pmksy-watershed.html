Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (2022-2024)

Capacity Building Training Sessions
Soil & Water Conservation
Community Participation
Micro-Irrigation Techniques

Program Overview

The Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) is a centrally sponsored scheme implemented by Coodu Trust in collaboration with the District Watershed Development Agency. The program's primary objective is to improve the productive potential of rainfed and degraded lands through an integrated watershed management approach. It focuses on strengthening community-based institutions, promoting sustainable livelihoods, and ensuring long-term agricultural sustainability for farmers in the project areas.

During the 2022–2024 period, the program's implementation was focused on extensive capacity-building initiatives. The geographical scope covered several districts, including Dindigul, Ramanathapuram, Thoothukudi, Virudhunagar, Perambalur, Krishnagiri, and Dharmapuri. The target beneficiaries were a diverse group of local stakeholders, including members of watershed committees, farmers, and Self-Help Group (SHG) members, who are crucial for the sustainable management of local resources.

Key Achievements & Activities (2022–2024)

Total Beneficiaries Trained

A total of 2,408 individuals received training, equipping them with modern agricultural techniques and sustainable practices.

Capacity Building

The core of the program involved focused block-level training sessions on critical topics, including:

  • Roles and Responsibilities of Watershed Committees for effective governance
  • Advanced Soil and Water Conservation Techniques
  • Skill Development for establishing agriculture-related Micro-Enterprises
  • Sustainable practices like Vermicomposting and Organic Farming
  • Water conservation through Micro-Irrigation Techniques for field crops

Exposure Visits

Organized training-cum-exposure visits to renowned agricultural institutions to provide participants with practical knowledge of modern cropping and water management practices. Institutions visited included:

  • KVK Gandhigram
  • TNAU Coimbatore
  • TNAU Killikulam
  • Horticulture College, Periyakulam

Program Impact

The initiative has led to improved awareness and adoption of soil and water conservation techniques, strengthened community-led management of natural resources through empowered watershed committees, and created new economic opportunities through the promotion of micro-enterprises and sustainable farming.
