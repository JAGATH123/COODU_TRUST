Programme Overview

Under the Central Sector Scheme for Promotion of 10,000 Farmers Producer Organisations (FPOs), the Government of India aims to ensure economies of scale for small and marginal farmers through collective action. Towards achieving this goal, Coodu Trust has been empanelled as a Cluster-Based Business Organization (CBBO) by the Tamil Nadu Small Farmers Agri-Business Consortium (TNSFAC).

🎯 Core Objectives

Establish and nurture Farmer Producer Companies that enable small farmers to aggregate their produce, improve bargaining power, access better market prices, and benefit from government schemes including financial support, subsidies, and equity grants — creating sustainable farmer-owned enterprises across Dindigul and Thiruvarur districts.

The Trust is actively engaged in fostering and developing FPOs in the Dindigul and Thiruvarur districts of Tamil Nadu. Four Farmer Producer Companies have been successfully registered and are operational, with a total of 1,103 farmers enrolled as members. Each FPO specialises in key agricultural trades relevant to its region, including garlic, maize, paddy, groundnut, coconut, onion, cotton, and green peas.

All four FPOs have successfully secured equity grants for their business operations, strengthening their financial base. Business activities have commenced, with training programmes empowering farmers in areas like agri-export, post-harvest management, and food processing. Coodu Trust ensures timely submission of progress reports to TNSFAC, maintaining transparency and accountability throughout the implementation.

Key Activities & Achievements

🏢

FPO Registration & Formation

Stats: 4 FPCs Registered | 2 Districts

Four Farmer Producer Companies were successfully registered — two in Dindigul district (Oddanchatram Agro and Kuriji Malai) and two in Thiruvarur district (Delta Vaduvur and Ponni Vanam). Each FPO was structured as a farmer-owned company with formal governance systems, elected boards, and operational business plans.

👨‍🌾

Farmer Mobilization & Enrollment

Stats: 1,103 Farmers Enrolled | 257 Women (24%)

Comprehensive farmer mobilization campaigns resulted in the enrollment of 1,103 farmers as members across the four FPOs. Notably, 257 members (24%) are women farmers, reflecting strong gender inclusion. Enrollment grew from 1,082 in 2022-2023 to 1,103 in 2023-2024, showing steady growth.

💰

Equity Grants & Financial Support

Achievement:
  • All 4 FPOs secured equity grants
  • Business operations commenced
  • Financial base strengthened for sustainability

All four FPOs successfully secured equity grants for their business operations from the government scheme, providing the critical seed capital needed to commence trading activities. This financial support has strengthened the FPOs' foundation and enabled them to begin serving their farmer members commercially.

🎓

Training & Capacity Building

Training Areas:
  • Agri-export procedures and opportunities
  • Post-harvest management techniques
  • Food processing and value addition
  • Agricultural marketing and development
  • FPO governance and management

Comprehensive training programmes were conducted for FPO members covering agri-export, post-harvest management, food processing, and agricultural marketing. These skills empower farmers to add value to their produce, access premium markets, and manage their collective enterprises professionally.

🌾

Crop-Specific Business Development

FPO Specialisations:
  • Oddanchatram: Maize, Copra (Coconut) & Onion
  • Kuriji Malai: Hill Garlic & Green Peas
  • Delta Vaduvur: Paddy & Groundnut
  • Ponni Vanam: Paddy & Cotton

Each FPO was developed with a focus on the key agricultural trades of its region, ensuring that collective business activities are grounded in crops that farmers already grow and understand. This crop-specific approach ensures relevance and immediate applicability.

📊

Compliance & Reporting

Governance Framework:
  • Timely progress reports to TNSFAC
  • Transparent financial management
  • Regular milestone tracking
  • Accountability to Central Government scheme objectives

Coodu Trust has maintained rigorous compliance standards, ensuring timely submission of progress reports to TNSFAC detailing milestones achieved and ongoing activities. This transparency and accountability reinforces the Trust's commitment to promoting FPOs in alignment with the national scheme objectives.

🤝 Sri Mugund Bakers & Traders Pvt. Ltd. Partnership

In addition to the TNSFAC scheme, Coodu Trust has initiated a separate FPO Promotion & Training Programme with the support of Sri Mugund Bakers & Traders Pvt. Ltd. in Kadavur Block, Karur District. This programme specifically focuses on empowering women farmers through:

📚 FPO Functioning Training

Two-day training for 250 women on FPO structure, roles, responsibilities, and government scheme benefits

🐄 Dairy Farming Training

300 women trained in 6 batches on fodder cultivation, livestock management, animal nutrition, and health management

🌾 Crop Management Training

Specialised sessions on crop management and fodder cultivation for integrated farming systems

👨‍🏫 Expert Involvement

Trainers from Veterinary Department, Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK), and Agriculture Department

💪 Impact: This CSR-supported programme has significantly contributed to women's empowerment in Kadavur Block, making women central to FPO decision-making processes and enhancing their agricultural knowledge and economic independence.
