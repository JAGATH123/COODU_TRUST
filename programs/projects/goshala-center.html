Program Mission

Promoting the welfare of stray cattle by providing shelter, nutrition, and medical care while creating a sustainable livelihood model for local communities through milk production, employment generation, and community education on animal welfare and responsible livestock care.

Programme Overview

Coodu Trust established the Goshala (Cow Care Center) at Velliyani, Karur District, with the dual aim of promoting the welfare of stray cattle and providing a sustainable livelihood for local communities. This initiative is a testament to Coodu Trust's commitment to animal welfare, rural development, and promoting dairy farming as a sustainable economic activity.

Core Objectives

Rescue and rehabilitate stray cattle from the streets, provide them with proper shelter, nutrition, and veterinary care, while simultaneously generating sustainable income through milk production — creating a model that combines animal welfare with economic viability and community development.

The Goshala is home to 32 stray cows, all rescued from the streets and brought to the centre for care and rehabilitation. These cows are provided with proper shelter, nutrition, and medical care to ensure their well-being. The centre is equipped with facilities to cater to the daily needs of the cows including feeding, health monitoring, and milking operations.

Beyond its primary welfare function, the Goshala generates 400-450 litres of milk daily, which is sold to a private company. This creates a sustainable source of income that supports the Goshala's operational costs while providing employment opportunities for local youth and women as caretakers, milk collectors, and administrative staff. The Goshala also serves as an educational resource where community members and school children learn about animal welfare and sustainable farming practices.

Key Activities & Achievements

🐄

Stray Cattle Rescue & Rehabilitation

32 Stray Cows Rescued

All 32 cows housed at the Goshala were rescued from the streets, where they posed hazards to both themselves and the public. Each cow is brought to the centre for care and rehabilitation, receiving proper shelter, nutrition, and medical attention. By removing stray cattle from streets, the Goshala contributes to both animal welfare and public safety.

🏥

Veterinary Care & Health Management

Care Protocols:
  • Regular monitoring by veterinary professionals
  • Necessary medical treatment and vaccinations
  • Preventive care and disease management
  • Ongoing health monitoring systems

The cows are regularly monitored by veterinary professionals, ensuring they receive necessary medical treatment, vaccinations, and preventive care. This comprehensive health management system ensures the well-being of every animal and maintains the productivity of the herd for sustainable milk production.

🌾

Feeding & Nutrition Management

Nutrition Programme:
  • Balanced diet including green fodder, silage, and essential nutrients
  • Year-round feeding programme
  • Nutritional management for health and productivity
  • Clean, spacious housing enclosures

A carefully designed balanced diet is provided to every cow, including green fodder, silage, and other essential nutrients. This nutrition management ensures both animal health and optimal milk production, with cows housed in clean, spacious enclosures that provide safety and comfort.

🥛

Milk Production & Revenue

400-450 Litres/Day | Sold to Private Company

The Goshala produces 400-450 litres of high-quality milk daily from the rescued cows. This milk is sold to a private company, creating a sustainable revenue stream that funds the Goshala's operational costs including feeding, medical care, and staff salaries. The quality of milk is consistently high as the cows are well-cared for and healthy.

👷

Employment & Community Impact

Employment Created:
  • Farmhands and caretakers
  • Milk collectors and handlers
  • Administrative staff
  • Youth and women from local communities

The Goshala provides direct employment to local youth and women in various roles — caretakers, milk collectors, and administrative staff. This creates steady income opportunities in a rural area, contributing to the economic upliftment of the surrounding community while ensuring the centre is managed by people invested in the local area.

🎓

Education & Awareness

Community Engagement:
  • Educational visits for school children
  • Animal welfare awareness programmes
  • Sustainable farming practice demonstrations
  • Compassion for animals advocacy

The Goshala serves as an educational resource where community members, especially school children, learn about the importance of animal welfare and sustainable farming practices. The centre actively promotes compassion for animals and encourages responsible pet and livestock care in the community.

Economic Sustainability Model

The Goshala operates on a self-sustaining economic model:

💰 Revenue

400-450 litres milk/day sold to private dairy company generates consistent daily income

📊 Operational Costs Covered

  • Animal feeding (green fodder, silage, nutrients)
  • Veterinary care and medical treatment
  • Staff salaries (caretakers, milk collectors, admin)
  • Infrastructure maintenance

🌟 Community Benefits

  • Direct employment for local youth and women
  • Reduced stray cattle hazard on streets
  • Educational resource for community
  • Promotion of animal welfare values

This model demonstrates that animal welfare and economic viability are not mutually exclusive — the Goshala supports both the rescued animals and the local community through a sustainable dairy operation.

Future Expansion Plans

Connection to Coodu Trust's Dairy Heritage

The Goshala represents the latest chapter in Coodu Trust's 15+ year dairy development journey:

Period Initiative Scale
2008-2010 Calf rearing promotion under RMK/Mahalir Thittam 50 milch animals distributed, 120 sheep farms
2013-2014 Dairy farmer skill training 2,476 farm women across 6 districts
2014-2017 PMKVY/NSDC certified dairy training 1,099 farmers, 461 NSDC certified
2021-Present Goshala at Velliyani 32 rescued cows, 400-450L milk/day
2022-Present TDPCL Dairy FPC (KVB/SBGF) 500 women, 10 milk centres, 1,800L/day

The Goshala adds a unique animal welfare dimension to Coodu Trust's dairy portfolio — while all other programmes focus on enabling farmers to rear dairy animals, the Goshala directly cares for abandoned cattle while demonstrating that welfare and productivity go hand in hand.
