Programme Overview

Between 2018 and 2023, Coodu Trust partnered with several leading corporate entities to implement a series of high-impact Rainwater Harvesting (RWH) projects under their Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) mandates. These initiatives were strategically designed to address critical water scarcity issues, enhance agricultural resilience, and promote sustainable livelihoods within local communities.

🎯 Core Objectives

Improve water security by rejuvenating traditional water bodies, increasing groundwater recharge, and empowering communities with the knowledge and infrastructure for effective water management.

The implementation was carried out in close collaboration with corporate partners including TATA Power, ATC Tyres (through United Way, Mumbai), Krishna Mines, and Cheran Spinning Mills. The geographical focus was primarily the water-stressed Manur Block in Tirunelveli District and parts of Karur District.

A key pillar of the implementation strategy was active community participation, which involved forming local user groups and conducting extensive capacity-building programmes to ensure the long-term sustainability and ownership of the created assets.

Key Activities & Achievements (2018–2023)

🏞️

Water Body Rejuvenation

11 Traditional Tanks Desilted

A major focus was the desilting of traditional water tanks to restore their storage capacity across projects supported by TATA Power and Krishna Mines, significantly enhancing water availability for agricultural communities.

⚡

Groundwater Recharge

A significant number of recharge structures were constructed to augment groundwater levels across various project sites:

  • • 118 Recharge Shafts - Deep structures for groundwater augmentation
  • • 25 Baby Ponds - Small water collection structures
  • • 90 Meters Trench Banding - Erosion control measures
🏠

Household-Level Harvesting

125 Rooftop RWH Systems

The programme extended to individual households, with rooftop rainwater harvesting systems installed to provide families with a direct and sustainable source of water for domestic use.

🤝

Community Empowerment

👥 14,000+ Farmers Benefited

Direct beneficiaries through improved water availability and training

🔧 7 User Groups Formed

Local management structures for long-term sustainability

📈 30% Area Expansion

Cultivable area increased, enabling multi-cropping practices

🌳

Afforestation and Greening

59,850 Trees & Seeds Planted

Extensive tree plantation drives were conducted to improve the local ecosystem and prevent soil erosion, including horticultural species and drought-resistant varieties like Palmyrah.

🌱 Measurable Impact

  • Water table increased by 5-10 feet
  • Enhanced ecosystem resilience and biodiversity
