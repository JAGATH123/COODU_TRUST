Project Overview

The Capacity Building of Water Users Associations (WUAs) for Participatory Irrigation Management (PIM) was implemented under the Tamil Nadu Irrigated Agriculture Modernization and Water-Bodies Restoration and Management (TNIAMWARM) Project from 2011–2014.

🎯 Primary Objective

Transition the management of local irrigation systems to the farmers themselves, empowering them to operate and maintain their water resources efficiently, equitably, and sustainably through comprehensive institutional capacity building.

This initiative was a critical "software" component that complemented the "hardware" (infrastructure restoration) of the broader TNIAMWARM project. The project aimed to create comprehensive awareness among farmers about the TNIAMWARM project objectives and the legal framework of the Tamil Nadu Farmers' Management of Irrigation Systems (TNFMIS) Act.

Key goals included training WUA members on technical and administrative aspects of water management, promoting modern water-saving agricultural technologies, and ensuring long-term sustainability of irrigation infrastructure through community ownership and responsibility.

🎯 Key Goals

📚 Awareness Creation: Comprehensive understanding of TNIAMWARM project and TNFMIS Act
🎓 Technical Training: Modern water-saving technologies and agricultural productivity enhancement
🤝 Community Ownership: Foster sense of responsibility for long-term irrigation infrastructure sustainability

Key Activities & Achievements (2011–2014)

📚

Awareness Creation

  • 🎯 TNIAMWARM Project Goals: Extensive campaigns on project objectives and expected outcomes
  • ⚖️ TNFMIS Act Education: Legal framework and procedures for participatory irrigation management
  • 👩‍💼 Women Participation: Encouraging active involvement in WUA activities and governance
🔧

Technical Training for Farmers

  • 💧 Water Management: Micro-irrigation, sprinkler systems, and fertigation technologies
  • 🌱 Production Technologies: Improved crop techniques for various agricultural systems
  • 🌿 Organic Farming: Sustainable and environmentally-friendly agricultural practices
🏛️

Institutional Capacity Building

Specialized training for WUA managing committees and four functional sub-committees on governance and operations:

  • 📊 Governance Training: Leadership and decision-making processes
  • 💰 Financial Management: Budget planning and resource allocation
  • 🔧 O&M Training: Operation and maintenance of irrigation assets
📈

Achievements & Outcomes

💧 39% Irrigation Increase

Fully irrigated area expanded across TNIAMWARM sub-basins

📊 23-47% Yield Increase

Significant productivity improvements across various crops

🤝 Strengthened Institutions

Functional, self-governing WUAs for sustainable water management
