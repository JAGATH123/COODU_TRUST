Initiative Overview

The MI-Tank Initiative was a comprehensive Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) programme implemented during 2022–2023 with the support of United Way, Mumbai. The initiative was designed to address critical water security and livelihood challenges in the Manur Block of Tirunelveli District.

🎯 Core Objectives

Rejuvenate local water bodies and promote sustainable agricultural practices, thereby enhancing community resilience and agricultural productivity in the region through comprehensive infrastructure development and community empowerment.

Coodu Trust served as the on-ground implementing agency, translating the CSR goals into tangible community-led action. This initiative adopted a holistic approach, integrating physical infrastructure development with intensive community capacity building.

By focusing on restoring traditional water systems (tanks and ponds) and equipping farmers and rural youth with modern skills, the programme aimed to create a sustainable ecosystem for water management and agricultural innovation, aligning with broader CSR goals of fostering long-term rural development.

Key Activities & Achievements (2022–2023)

💧

Water Infrastructure Restoration

25 Cattle Ponds
Multiple Recharge Shafts

Constructed and repaired cattle ponds to ensure reliable water sources for livestock, supporting animal health and local dairy activities while installing groundwater recharge structures.

🌿

Ecosystem Restoration

  • 🌿 Invasive Species Clearing: Large-scale removal of Prosopis Juliflora plants from local ponds, restoring water bodies' storage capacity
  • 🌳 Native Tree Plantation: Replaced invasive species with ecologically beneficial native trees including fruit-bearing varieties
  • 🐦 Biodiversity Enhancement: Revitalized native biodiversity and improved soil quality for long-term ecosystem health
