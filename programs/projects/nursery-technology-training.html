Nursery Technology Training

Seedling Production Skills for Forest & Horticultural Crops (2013-2014)

🎯 Program Mission

Equipping rural youth with professional nursery technology skills for raising quality seedlings of forest and horticultural crop species — creating skilled nursery professionals who can support afforestation programmes, supply the horticulture industry, and build sustainable self-employment through nursery enterprises that contribute to Tamil Nadu's green cover expansion.

Programme Overview

Creating tree cover in non-conventional forest areas through social forestry activities — farm forestry, roadside plantations, canal bank plantations — is one of the most important climate-resilient strategies for rural Tamil Nadu. However, the quality of saplings planted directly determines the success of any plantation programme. Recognising this critical need, Coodu Trust organised comprehensive nursery technology training programmes for rural youth in 2013-2014.

🎯 Core Rationale

The quality of saplings decides the success of plantations. Poor-quality seedlings lead to high mortality, wasted resources, and failed afforestation efforts. By training rural youth in professional nursery technology, Coodu Trust simultaneously addresses two challenges — ensuring high-quality planting material for green cover expansion while creating skilled self-employment opportunities for rural youth.

A total of 300 rural youth were trained through 15 training programmes, each of 10 days duration, across Karur, Dindigul, and Virudhunagar districts. The curriculum covered seedling production of both forest tree species with multipurpose utility and horticultural fruit, ornamental, and flower crops — providing trainees with versatile skills applicable across the nursery industry.

The training included vegetative propagation technologies essential for raising fruit crops, ornamentals, and flower crops, in addition to seed-based propagation for forest species. This dual competency — both sexual and asexual plant propagation — makes the trained youth valuable to commercial nurseries, government afforestation programmes, and self-run nursery enterprises.

Key Activities & Achievements

🌱

Seedling Production — Forest Species

Multipurpose Forest Tree Species

Training covered the nursery technology of forest tree species with multipurpose utility — timber, fuelwood, fodder, and environmental services. Youth learned seed collection, treatment, germination techniques, potting media preparation, and seedling care for species commonly used in social forestry programmes including farm forestry, roadside plantations, and canal bank afforestation.

🍎

Vegetative Propagation — Fruit Crops

Horticultural Fruit Varieties

Specialised training in vegetative propagation technologies — budding, grafting, layering, and cutting — required for raising true-to-type fruit crop plants. These asexual propagation skills are essential for producing commercially viable fruit plant material where seed propagation would not maintain varietal characteristics. This makes trained youth valuable to both commercial nurseries and fruit farming programmes.

🌺

Ornamental & Flower Crops

Landscape & Commercial Floriculture

Youth were trained in propagation and nursery management of ornamental plants and flower crops for landscape gardening, potted plant production, and commercial floriculture. These high-value horticultural products have strong urban market demand and provide excellent income opportunities for nursery entrepreneurs.

🏢

Nursery Enterprise Development

Self-Employment Potential

  • • Low capital investment for starting a nursery
  • • High demand from afforestation programmes
  • • Government nursery supply contracts available
  • • Private sector landscape and gardening market
  • • Year-round production and income potential

The 10-day intensive training equipped youth with all skills needed to establish and run independent nursery enterprises. The combination of forest species (high volume, government demand) and horticultural species (high value, private market) provides diversified income streams for nursery entrepreneurs.

Training Curriculum

🌾 Seed-Based Propagation (Forest Species)

  • ✓ Seed collection, extraction, and storage techniques
  • ✓ Seed treatment and dormancy breaking methods
  • ✓ Germination media and bed preparation
  • ✓ Seedling transplanting and hardening
  • ✓ Nursery bed management and irrigation

🌿 Vegetative Propagation (Horticultural Crops)

  • ✓ Budding techniques — T-budding, patch budding, chip budding
  • ✓ Grafting methods — cleft, whip, approach, veneer grafting
  • ✓ Layering — air layering, ground layering, mound layering
  • ✓ Cutting propagation — softwood, semi-hardwood, hardwood cuttings
  • ✓ Root stock selection and management

🏗️ Nursery Management

  • ✓ Site selection and nursery layout design
  • ✓ Potting media preparation and soil mixtures
  • ✓ Irrigation and drainage systems
  • ✓ Pest and disease management in nursery
  • ✓ Hardening and acclimatisation of seedlings
  • ✓ Record keeping and business management

🌳 Species-Specific Modules

  • ✓ Forest trees with multipurpose utility (timber, fuel, fodder)
  • ✓ Commercial fruit crops (mango, guava, sapota, citrus)
  • ✓ Ornamental and landscape plants
  • ✓ Flower crops for commercial cultivation

Why Nursery Skills Matter for Tamil Nadu

🌳 Afforestation Demand

Tamil Nadu has ambitious afforestation targets under multiple government schemes (TNCWP, IWDP, DPAP, social forestry) all requiring millions of quality seedlings annually. Trained nursery professionals are essential to meet this demand.

🌺 Horticulture Industry Growth

Tamil Nadu's horticulture sector is expanding rapidly. Commercial nurseries supplying grafted fruit plants, ornamental varieties, and flower crops are a growing business opportunity.

🌍 Climate Resilience

Tree planting in non-conventional forest areas — roadside, community lands, farm boundaries — is a critical climate adaptation strategy. Every quality seedling that survives contributes to carbon sequestration, soil protection, and microclimate improvement.

💼 Self-Employment Potential

A nursery enterprise requires relatively low capital, uses locally available materials, and has guaranteed demand from both government programmes and private markets. The 10-day training provides sufficient skills to start a small nursery immediately.
