Programme Overview

The Bamboo Cultivation Demonstration Programme, conducted in 2009–2010, was a collaborative initiative designed to showcase modern and sustainable forestry practices to the farming community. The primary objective was to demonstrate the field performance of commercially important bamboo species by using advanced propagation techniques.

🎯 Dual Purpose Initiative

Designed to promote bamboo as a viable and eco-friendly agricultural option that could enhance land productivity and provide a sustainable livelihood source for farmers.

The programme was implemented as a technical demonstration in partnership with specialized research institutions. By showcasing the growth of different bamboo species from both micro-propagated (tissue culture) and macro-propagated planting stock, the project provided farmers with practical insights into modern cultivation methods.

This approach was intended to encourage the adoption of bamboo cultivation as part of a diversified and resilient farming system, creating sustainable opportunities for environmental conservation and rural economic development.
