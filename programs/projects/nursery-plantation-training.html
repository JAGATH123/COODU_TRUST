Nursery and raising plantation training programme

A Coodu Trust skill-development initiative that trained women's Self-Help Groups in nursery management and plantation techniques across Dindigul, Trichy and Karur districts of Tamil Nadu.

  • Trust initiative
  • 2003–2004
  • Completed
3 Districts covered
5 Nurseries established
26,93,250 Trees planted statewide (Trust cumulative)
35,000+ Hectares covered (Trust cumulative)

The project

What this training set out to do

Project mission

Empowering rural women through skill development in nursery management and plantation techniques while creating sustainable income-generating opportunities for environmental conservation.

The Nursery and Raising Plantation Training Programme was implemented by Coodu Trust in 2003–2004 as part of its skill development and livelihood initiatives. Described as a foundation project, it targeted women's Self-Help Groups — SHG members, rural women farmers and aspiring entrepreneurs — across 3 districts: Dindigul, Trichy and Karur.

Delivered as part of a broader entrepreneurship training strategy, the programme equipped groups with practical skills to establish community-based nurseries and contribute to local afforestation and watershed development efforts. Per Coodu Trust records, 5 nurseries were established, including a state-of-the-art Hi-tech Nursery in Karur district.

The training's legacy extends well beyond its two-year run: it contributed to Coodu Trust's cumulative plantation success of 26,93,250 trees planted across 14 districts, covering 35,000+ hectares in Tamil Nadu.

  • Women's economic empowerment
  • Environmental conservation
  • Sustainable livelihood creation
  • Community nurseries

What the training covered

  1. Nursery management Techniques for establishing and managing nurseries — seed selection, sowing and caring for saplings.
  2. Plantation techniques Best practices for planting saplings to support sustainable agriculture and conservation goals.
  3. Entrepreneurship training Part of a broader strategy targeting women's SHGs in Dindigul, Trichy and Karur districts.
  4. Community nursery establishment Practical skills to set up community-based nurseries that feed local afforestation and watershed work.

Why this work matters

The challenge on the ground — and our answer

The challenge

Willing hands without the technical skills

Rural women's Self-Help Groups were ready to work — but without training in nursery raising, they could not turn green work into a viable income-generating enterprise.

Our response

Hands-on skills, framed as enterprise

Training in nursery management and plantation techniques for SHG members, rural women farmers and aspiring entrepreneurs across Dindigul, Trichy and Karur — delivered within a broader entrepreneurship strategy, with community nurseries identified as a key income-generating activity.

The challenge

Afforestation without a sapling supply

Plantation and watershed drives stall without a steady local supply of healthy saplings — communities lacked nurseries of their own to feed the work.

Our response

Five nurseries, one of them Hi-tech

5 nurseries established, including a state-of-the-art Hi-tech Nursery in Karur district — centers that went on to support Coodu Trust's plantation of 26,93,250 trees across 14 districts of Tamil Nadu.

Outcomes

What the project changed

Independent capability

Built the capacity of women's SHGs to undertake nursery and plantation activities independently — skills that stay in the village.

Enterprise development

The training served as a foundation for viable economic enterprises — community nurseries emerged as a key income-generating activity for women's groups.

Long-term impact

The 5 nurseries, including the Hi-tech nursery in Karur, became centers of excellence — contributing to the Trust's cumulative 26,93,250 trees planted across 14 districts covering 35,000+ hectares.
