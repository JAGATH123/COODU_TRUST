Programme Overview

The Nursery and Raising Plantation Training Programme, conducted during 2003–2004, was a key initiative by Coodu Trust aimed at empowering rural women through skill development. The primary objective of the programme was to train women's Self-Help Groups (SHGs) in nursery management and plantation techniques.

🎯 Dual Purpose Initiative

Designed to promote sustainable agricultural and environmental practices while simultaneously creating viable income-generating opportunities for participants.

The programme was implemented as part of a broader entrepreneurship training strategy, targeting women's SHGs in the districts of Dindigul, Trichy, and Karur. By equipping these groups with practical skills, the training aimed to foster self-sufficiency, enabling them to establish community-based nurseries and contribute to local afforestation and watershed development efforts.

This approach directly supported both the economic empowerment of women and the ecological health of their communities, creating a sustainable model for environmental conservation through women-led enterprises.

Training Content

The training modules focused on imparting essential skills for sustainable agricultural and environmental practices:

🌱 Nursery Management

Techniques for establishing and managing nurseries, including seed selection, sowing, and caring for saplings.

🌳 Plantation Techniques

Best practices for planting saplings to support sustainable agriculture and environmental conservation goals.
