Training on Coconut Climbing

Rural Youth Livelihood through Professional Palm Climbing Skills (2013-2014)

🎯 Program Mission

Addressing the critical shortage of skilled labour for coconut palm cleaning and harvesting in Tamil Nadu by training rural youth in professional palm climbing techniques using modern climbing devices, creating a high-income livelihood opportunity that directly addresses one of the biggest constraints in the coconut farming sector.

Programme Overview

Coconut is one of the most favoured crops of all farmers in Tamil Nadu. However, the area under coconut cultivation has been diminishing and productivity has declined. The hindering factors are multifarious — frequent drought and fluctuating rainfall are primary concerns, but the most acute constraint is the severe shortage of skilled labour for palm cleaning and nut harvesting across all coconut-growing areas.

⚠️ Core Problem

Labour shortage, especially of skilled workers for palm cleaning and harvesting nuts, has become exceptionally high across all coconut cultivation areas in Tamil Nadu. Without regular harvesting and palm cleaning, coconut productivity declines further, tree health deteriorates, and farmers face significant income losses — creating a vicious cycle of declining coconut cultivation.

Coodu Trust designed a skill-oriented training programme specifically for rural youth, equipping them with professional palm climbing and cleaning skills using two types of modern climbing devices: one designed by Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU) and another popularised by the Coconut Development Board (CDB). Each training programme was tailored for 5 days duration, providing intensive hands-on practice with these devices.

A total of 220 rural youth were trained through 7 training programmes. The programme has produced tangible livelihood outcomes — 72 of the trained youth have adopted coconut climbing as their primary profession, now earning up to ₹750 per day by charging ₹15 to ₹20 per tree. This represents one of the highest daily income rates achievable through skill-based rural employment.

Key Activities & Achievements

🎓

Structured 5-Day Training Programme

7 Programmes | 5 Days Each | 220 Youth

Seven structured training programmes were conducted, each tailored for 5 days duration, providing intensive skill development in palm climbing techniques. The training combined classroom instruction on safety, tree biology, and equipment handling with extensive practical sessions on actual coconut palms, ensuring participants achieved proficiency before completion.

🔧

Modern Climbing Device Training

Two Devices Taught:

  • • TNAU Climbing Device — designed by Tamil Nadu Agricultural University for safe, efficient palm climbing
  • • CDB Climbing Device — popularised by the Coconut Development Board as a national standard

Youth were trained on two complementary climbing devices — the TNAU-designed device and the Coconut Development Board device. Both devices enable safe, efficient climbing without the physical strain and risk of traditional barefoot climbing, making the profession accessible to a wider range of youth and significantly reducing injury risk.

🥥

Palm Cleaning & Harvesting Skills

Skill Areas:

  • • Safe palm climbing techniques using mechanical devices
  • • Palm crown cleaning and dead frond removal
  • • Nut harvesting at optimal maturity
  • • Tree health assessment and pest identification
  • • Multiple tree servicing efficiency

Training covered the complete spectrum of coconut palm maintenance — from climbing and crown cleaning to nut harvesting. Youth learned to assess tree health, remove dead fronds that harbour pests, and harvest coconuts at the right stage of maturity for maximum value. These skills make trained climbers valuable service providers to coconut farmers.

💰

Livelihood Impact — ₹750/Day

Employment Outcomes:

  • • 72 youth adopted climbing as profession (33% conversion rate)
  • • Earning up to ₹750/day
  • • Charging ₹15-20 per tree
  • • Self-employed with no capital investment required
  • • Year-round demand from coconut farmers

The most powerful outcome: 72 of the 220 trained youth have taken up coconut climbing as their primary livelihood, earning up to ₹750 per day. At ₹15-20 per tree, a skilled climber services 40-50 trees daily. This self-employment requires zero capital investment — only the climbing device and acquired skill — making it one of the most efficient skill-to-income conversion programmes.

Why This Programme Matters — The Coconut Crisis

Tamil Nadu's coconut sector faces a compounding crisis that this training directly addresses:

⚠️ The Problem

  • • Coconut area is diminishing across Tamil Nadu
  • • Productivity has declined due to irregular maintenance
  • • Frequent drought and fluctuating rainfall stress trees
  • • Severe skilled labour shortage for palm cleaning and harvesting
  • • Existing climbers are aging with no younger generation replacing them
  • • Without regular harvesting, farmers lose income and abandon coconut cultivation

✅ The Solution — Trained Youth Climbers

  • • Modern climbing devices make the profession safer and less physically demanding
  • • 5-day training creates job-ready professionals immediately
  • • ₹750/day earning potential attracts youth who would otherwise migrate to cities
  • • Self-employment with zero capital investment
  • • Year-round demand — coconuts are harvested every 45-60 days
  • • Each trained climber can service 100+ farming households

📈 The Multiplier Effect

  • • 72 active climbers service thousands of coconut farmers
  • • Regular harvesting improves tree productivity and farmer income
  • • Prevents further decline of coconut cultivation in the region
  • • Keeps rural youth employed in their communities rather than migrating

Technical Partners

🏛️ Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)

TNAU designed one of the climbing devices used in the training programme. As India's premier agricultural university, TNAU's involvement ensured the device was ergonomically designed, tested for safety, and optimised for Tamil Nadu's coconut palm varieties. TNAU is also listed as a key technical partner in Coodu Trust's broader agricultural programme portfolio.

🥥 Coconut Development Board (CDB)

The Coconut Development Board, a statutory body under the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India, popularised the second climbing device used in the training. CDB's national-level promotion of mechanical climbing devices is part of India's strategy to address the coconut harvesting labour crisis.
