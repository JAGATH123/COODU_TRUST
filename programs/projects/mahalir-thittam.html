Mahalir Thittam Project

Economic & Social Empowerment of Women through SHGs, Micro-Credit & Livestock Development (2006-2018)

🎯 Program Mission

Promoting economic development and social empowerment of poor and disadvantaged women through a network of Self-Help Groups, providing micro-credit for income generation and asset creation, facilitating bank linkage for livestock rearing, and building functional literacy, communication, and leadership skills — enabling women to participate fully and actively in decision-making at the household and community level.

Programme Overview

The Tamil Nadu Women's Development Project, known as "Mahalir Thittam," is implemented by the Tamil Nadu Corporation for Women Development (TNCWD). Coodu Trust was selected as the approved NGO for Mahalir Thittam in Karur District, operating across four blocks — Kadavur, Thogamalai, Aravakurichi, and Paramathi — from January 2006 onwards. The project value was ₹39,60,000 with a total staff deployment of 180 staff-months.

🎯 Core Objectives

Promote economic development and social empowerment of poor and disadvantaged women through four pillars: Social Empowerment (awareness building and decision-making participation), Economic Empowerment (savings, micro-credit, and income generation), Capacity Building (functional literacy, leadership, and communication skills), and Financial Services (SHG formation, bank linkage, and revolving fund access).

The programme was oriented to empower women to work together with men as equal partners, inspiring a new generation of women and men to work together for equality, sustainable development, and communal harmony. Coodu Trust performed this programme among Women Self-Help Groups by demonstrating and replicating participatory approaches for thrift and savings with effective utilisation of credit.

Under this programme, Coodu Trust formed 1,345 men and women Self-Help Groups across 11 districts of Tamil Nadu. These SHGs were enabled for revolving fund and economic assistance through SGSY, IWDP, Mahalir Thittam, NABARD, TAHDCO, and other nationalised banks. The total financial assistance of nearly ₹6 crore was facilitated to SHG members. The poor landless labourers who once struggled for their livelihoods wisely used the money for livelihood development through petty trades, livestock development, and small-scale cottage industries.

Key Activities & Achievements

👩‍🤝‍👩

SHG Formation & Management

1,345 SHGs | 11 Districts

Coodu Trust was instrumental in forming 1,345 men and women Self-Help Groups across 11 districts of Tamil Nadu. Each SHG evolved through processes of Group Formation (leading to Federation), Capital Formation through revolving funds, and Skill Development to motivate women to become entrepreneurs and leaders.

💰

Financial Assistance & Bank Linkage

~₹6 Crore | Multiple Schemes

SHG members were enabled for revolving fund and economic assistance through SGSY, IWDP, Mahalir Thittam, NABARD, TAHDCO, and nationalised banks. Total financial assistance of nearly ₹6 crore was facilitated. Members used funds for petty trades, livestock development, and small-scale cottage industries, drastically improving their quality of life.

🐄

Cow Rearing Bank Linkage

67 SHGs | ₹1.33 Crore Bank Loan

Under the micro-finance management component, 67 SHGs of Karur District were linked to nationalised banks for obtaining a financial loan of ₹1.33 crore specifically for rearing cows. This enabled women to acquire milch animals for dairy income generation, connecting micro-credit with productive asset creation.

🎓

Financial Inclusion Training

50 SHGs | Kadavur & Thogamalai Blocks

Training on financial inclusion was provided to 50 SHGs of Kadavur and Thogamalai blocks, covering savings management, internal lending practices, bank account operations, government scheme access, and financial literacy to strengthen women's economic decision-making capability.

🌾

Entrepreneurship & Skill Training

Training Delivered (2009-2010):

  • • Dairy development: 52 women beneficiaries
  • • Tailoring: 47 women beneficiaries
  • • Nursery management: 27 women beneficiaries
  • • Vermicomposting: 32 women beneficiaries
  • • Mushroom production: 43 women beneficiaries
  • • Total: 201 women trained in entrepreneurship skills

Entrepreneurship trainings were delivered across multiple skill areas, enabling women to start micro-enterprises. The major viable economic activities that trained SHGs undertook include Community Herbal Nursery, Community Nurseries, Vermi-Casting/Composting Production Centre, and Mushroom Production Centre.

🐑

Livestock Enterprise Development

Enterprises Established (through Mahalir Thittam financial assistance):

  • • 120 commercial sheep farms in Kadavur Block, Karur
  • • 10 commercial goat farms (with UVTRC Karur technical support)
  • • 4 backyard poultry units at Mettupatty village, 25 women trained
  • • Calf rearing encouraged for small dairy farmers

Through the financial linkages established by Mahalir Thittam, multiple livestock enterprises were created in the Kadavur region. Sheep farming with 120 commercial farms became a significant livelihood source, while goat farming was established with technical backup from the University Veterinary Training and Research Centre (UVTRC) at Karur.

Convergence Monitoring Component

NREGS Convergence Monitoring

The Mahalir Thittam project also included a convergence monitoring component with NREGS (National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme):

📊 Monitoring Methods

Monitoring convergence activities using questionnaires, FDG, Participatory Impact Monitoring and Assessment

🔄 Comparative Analysis

Intra and inter-district comparisons to highlight models and practices for replication

🏗️ Asset Creation Tracking

Monitoring durable assets created and value addition through NREGS convergence for sustainable livelihoods

📈 Impact Indicators

Tracking physical assets (road length, tank size), enduring outcomes (area irrigated, villages connected), and improvements in cultivable area, irrigation potential, groundwater recharge, and crop production
