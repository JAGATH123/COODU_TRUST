Solid Waste Management Program (DCMC)

Comprehensive Urban Waste Management Operations for Dindigul City (2022-Present)

🎯 Program Mission

Managing the complete solid waste management operations for Dindigul City Municipal Corporation across all 48 wards, encompassing door-to-door waste collection, segregation, transportation, composting, recycling, and landfill remediation — transforming urban waste management through systematic community engagement and scientific waste processing.

Programme Overview

Since 2022, Coodu Trust has been entrusted with managing the entire solid waste management operations of the Dindigul City Municipal Corporation (DCMC) — one of the most significant urban service delivery contracts undertaken by any NGO in Tamil Nadu. This partnership represents Coodu Trust's transition from rural watershed management to comprehensive urban environmental management, leveraging 25 years of community mobilisation expertise.

🎯 Core Objectives

Achieve 100% waste collection coverage across all 48 municipal wards, establish scientific waste segregation and processing systems, ensure dignified working conditions for 400 sanitation workers, and transition Dindigul towards a circular-economy approach to solid waste management.

The contract encompasses the full spectrum of solid waste management responsibilities: door-to-door collection from 54,000 households (including 28 slum clusters), waste segregation at source, transportation to processing facilities, composting of organic waste, recycling of dry waste, and supervision of Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs) and Micro Composting Centres (MCCs). Coodu Trust manages a workforce of approximately 400 sanitation workers who collectively handle roughly 90 tons of waste daily.

This operation is funded by the Dindigul City Municipal Corporation with a total contract value of approximately ₹7.1 crore, making DCMC the single largest funder of Coodu Trust's operations. The daily reporting structure includes tracking kilograms of waste collected, roll call monitoring of workers, and GPS tracking of collection vehicles.

Key Achievements & Activities

🚛

Door-to-Door Collection

54,000 Households
Daily Operations

Comprehensive door-to-door waste collection service covering all 54,000 households across 48 wards of Dindigul city, including 28 slum clusters. Collection operations run daily with route-optimised vehicle deployment and GPS tracking for accountability and efficiency.

♻️

Waste Segregation & Processing

Current Status:

  • • ~100 MT/day waste generated in Dindigul
  • • 100% collection mechanism operational
  • • 30% currently processed scientifically (gap being addressed)
  • • Segregation compliance currently below 40%

Source-level segregation drives conducted across all wards with awareness campaigns targeting households to improve the current below-40% compliance rate. The aim is to achieve 100% three-bin segregation across all wards.

👷

Workforce Management

400 Workers
360+ Sanitation Workers

Management of approximately 400 workers including daily roll call monitoring, deployment across wards, vehicle route optimisation, and supervision. Currently, 360+ sanitation workers operate in largely informal conditions — formalisation and dignified working conditions are a key focus area.

🏗️

Infrastructure Supervision

Facilities Managed:

  • • Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs)
  • • Micro Composting Centres (MCCs)
  • • Collection vehicles fleet
  • • Composting units

Supervision and management of waste processing infrastructure including MRFs for dry waste sorting and recycling, MCCs for organic waste composting, and the collection vehicle fleet ensuring systematic waste transportation.

📢

Community Awareness

60,000 Individuals
School Students Reached

Extensive awareness campaigns on waste segregation at source conducted across all wards, reaching over 60,000 individuals and school students. Campaigns focus on three-bin segregation practices, composting, and the importance of reducing waste at source.

🦟

Dengue Eradication Programme

Additional Responsibility:

  • • Door-to-door anti-larval activities
  • • Fogging operations across all wards
  • • Coordination with health department

Beyond waste management, the DCMC contract includes public health interventions — specifically door-to-door anti-larval and fogging activities as part of the Dengue Eradication Programme, demonstrating the integrated nature of urban environmental health management.

Emission Reduction & Environmental Impact

The systematic collection and scientific processing of 90 tons of waste daily directly contributes to emission reduction through several pathways:

🔥 Elimination of Open Burning

Systematic collection prevents uncontrolled waste burning, a major source of particulate matter and greenhouse gas emissions

🗑️ Landfill Diversion

Segregation and composting reduce the volume of waste reaching landfills, reducing methane emissions from anaerobic decomposition

🌱 Composting

Organic waste processing through composting reduces methane generation compared to landfill disposal

♻️ Recycling

Material recovery through MRFs reduces the need for virgin resource extraction and associated emissions

🚛 Systematic Transport

GPS-optimised collection routes reduce vehicle emissions from waste transportation
