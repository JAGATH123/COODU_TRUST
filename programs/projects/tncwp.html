TNCWP Overview

The Tamil Nadu Comprehensive Watershed Programme (TNCWP) was a state-sponsored initiative implemented by Coodu Trust between 2001 and 2005. The program was designed to address the specific ecological and agricultural challenges of the state by focusing on wasteland development and the sustainable management of natural resources. Its core objective was to restore ecological balance by conserving soil, water, and vegetation, thereby improving agricultural productivity and creating sustainable livelihood opportunities for rural communities.

🎯 Core Strategy

Implementation of integrated watershed management to conserve natural resources, restore ecological balance, and improve the productive potential of degraded lands through comprehensive wasteland development approach.

The implementation of TNCWP was deeply rooted in community participation. Coodu Trust acted as the primary implementing agency, working in close coordination with various government departments, including Agriculture, Horticulture, and Forestry. The program was rolled out across five key districts: Dindigul, Theni, Tirunelveli, Namakkal, and Salem.

A key feature of the program was the establishment of grassroots institutions, including Village Development Associations, Area Groups, User Groups, and Self-Help Groups (SHGs), to ensure that local communities were at the center of planning and executing the watershed development plans.
