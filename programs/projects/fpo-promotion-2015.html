Farmer Producer Organisation (FPO) Promotion

Awareness, Formation & Management Training for Farmer Collectives (2015-2016)

🎯 Program Mission

Creating awareness among farming communities on the benefits of Farmer Producer Organisations, their formation and management processes, and the opportunities available through government agencies — empowering small and marginal farmers to collectively market their products and practice profitable agriculture.

Programme Overview

The Farmer Producer Organisation (FPO) Promotion programme was implemented by Coodu Trust during 2015-2016, aligned with the Government of India's emphasis on promoting FPOs for the benefit of small and marginal farmers. The programme recognised that individual farmers are vulnerable to market exploitation and that collective action through FPOs could arm them to practice profitable agriculture and market their products directly to customers.

🎯 Core Objectives

Create comprehensive awareness among farming communities about FPO concepts, guide farmers through the formation and management processes of producer organisations, and connect them with government support schemes — laying the foundation for Coodu Trust's subsequent large-scale FPO promotion work.

The programme was implemented across 10 villages in 3 blocks of Dindigul district, specifically targeting farmers growing four key crops: Maize, Cotton, Vegetables, and Mango. Through a series of awareness sessions, training workshops, and an exposure visit programme, approximately 600 farmers gained knowledge about the FPO model and its potential benefits for their agricultural livelihoods.

A highlight of the programme was the exposure visit organised for 30 farmers to successful, operational FPOs in Kannivadi and Palani areas of Dindigul district. This practical exposure allowed farmers to see first-hand how FPOs function, understand the benefits of collective marketing, and interact with fellow farmers who had already benefited from the FPO model.

This foundation initiative directly laid the groundwork for Coodu Trust's subsequent empanelment as a Cluster-Based Business Organisation (CBBO) by TNSFAC under the Central Government's 10,000 FPO Scheme, which has since resulted in the establishment of 4 registered Farmer Producer Companies with over 1,100 enrolled farmer members.

Programme Legacy — From Foundation to Scale

2015-2016

Foundation Awareness Programme

600 farmers reached across 10 villages, 3 blocks in Dindigul district. Created comprehensive awareness on FPO concepts, formation, and management among Maize, Cotton, Vegetables, and Mango farmers.

2022-2023

CBBO Empanelment & FPC Registration

Coodu Trust empanelled as Cluster-Based Business Organisation (CBBO) by TNSFAC under Central Government's 10,000 FPO Scheme. 4 FPCs registered: Oddanchatram Agro, Kuriji Malai (Dindigul); Delta Vaduvur, Ponni Vanam (Thiruvarur).

2022-2023

Member Enrollment Phase

1,082 farmers enrolled (300 + 100 + 365 + 317 across 4 FPOs). 257 women members (24% women participation), demonstrating gender-inclusive farmer collective development.

2023-2024

Sustained Growth & Expansion

1,103 farmers enrolled (311 + 110 + 365 + 317). Continued member base strengthening and organizational capacity building across both Dindigul and Thiruvarur districts.

2023-2024

Equity Grants & Business Commencement

All 4 FPOs received equity grants from government agencies. Business operations commenced — FPCs began collective procurement, value addition, and marketing activities for member farmers.

2023-2024

Sri Mugund CSR Partnership

FPO & Dairy Training at Kadavur Block, Karur. 250 women received FPO formation and management training. 300 women trained in dairy enterprise development — expanding FPO model to livestock sector.

🏆 From 600 to 1,100+ Farmers

The 2015-2016 foundation programme's grassroots awareness-building approach proved its value, enabling Coodu Trust to scale up FPO promotion work nearly tenfold within seven years — from a single-district awareness initiative to a multi-district institutional FPO promotion programme under the Central Government's flagship scheme.
