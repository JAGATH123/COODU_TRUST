Program Overview

The Vermi-composting and Organic Farming Training program was a comprehensive initiative implemented by Coodu Trust from 2003 to 2005, designed to promote sustainable agricultural practices among farmers and women's Self-Help Groups (SHGs) across Tamil Nadu. This targeted program addressed the critical need to move away from chemical fertilizer dependency while enhancing soil fertility and agricultural productivity.

🎯 Primary Objectives

Enhance soil fertility and increase crop yields through sustainable practices, reduce input costs for farmers, and create new income-generating opportunities for rural women through eco-friendly farming solutions.

The program provided hands-on skill development in vermiculture and organic farming techniques, focusing on improving the long-term health of agricultural land while creating sustainable livelihoods for rural communities. This initiative was strategically integrated into broader watershed development and women's empowerment programs.

By targeting both farmers and women's SHGs, the program created a dual impact: agricultural sustainability and economic empowerment, particularly for women who gained valuable skills to start vermi-composting enterprises and strengthen their economic independence.
