IWDP Overview

The Integrated Watershed Development Programme (IWDP) was a government-sponsored scheme implemented by Coodu Trust from 2001 to 2005. The program was designed for the holistic development of watersheds, with a primary focus on conserving essential natural resources like soil, water, and biomass. By treating entire watershed areas, the IWDP aimed to restore ecological balance, improve the productivity of land, and create sustainable livelihood opportunities for the rural resource-poor, farmers, and other community members.

🎯 Core Strategy

Implementation of holistic watershed treatment to conserve essential natural resources, restore ecological balance, improve land productivity, and create sustainable livelihood opportunities for rural communities.

The implementation framework was built on a foundation of deep community participation. Coodu Trust served as the key implementing agency, rolling out the program across seven districts: Dindigul, Karur, Madurai, Theni, Trichy, Tirunelveli, and Sivagangai. The operational strategy involved mobilizing local communities and establishing a robust, three-tiered governance structure consisting of Watershed Associations, executive Watershed Committees, and numerous User Groups (UGs) and Self-Help Groups (SHGs).

This ensured that all planning and execution were participatory and aligned with the specific needs of the local population.

The initial phase (2001-2002) successfully formed 127 Watershed Associations and Committees, over 10,270 User Groups, and more than 7,340 Self-Help Groups, creating a vast network of community institutions.
