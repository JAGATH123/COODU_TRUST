Our agricultural mission

Creating sustainable agriculture

For over two decades, Coodu Trust has been empowering farmers and rural communities through sustainable agriculture practices in Tamil Nadu. Our comprehensive approach addresses farming challenges through science-based solutions, collective action and innovative agricultural technologies that ensure environmental sustainability and economic profitability.

We integrate traditional farming wisdom with modern agricultural techniques to create resilient farming systems. From farmer collectivization and organic practices to livestock management and market linkages, our programs have trained 8,250+ farmers, treated 4,660 animals and distributed 1,202 milch animals — building sustainable livelihoods that support both agricultural productivity and rural prosperity.

What this sector delivers

  1. Empowered farming communities Collective action, cooperatives and modern techniques for organized farmers.
  2. Diversified rural livelihoods Integrated livestock and agriculture systems for multiple income sources.
  3. Organic farming Chemical-free methods for environmental sustainability and health.
  4. Stronger value chains Agribusiness ventures, market linkages, and technology and knowledge transfer that build farmer resilience.
