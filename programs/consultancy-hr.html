Our consultancy mission

Professional consultancy and HR solutions

What sets us apart

We draw upon our 25+ years of field experience to provide practical, results-oriented solutions that enhance organizational capacity and program effectiveness.

Our consultancy and HR management services leverage our extensive experience in development programs to provide professional advisory, strategic planning and human resource solutions to 50+ organizations — NGOs, government agencies and development partners. These services help organizations optimize their operations, implement best practices and achieve their development goals more effectively.

The sector encompasses technology transfer and knowledge dissemination across 100+ development projects, strategic planning and advisory services for development programs, and comprehensive staffing solutions that have placed 500+ qualified professionals in development sector roles.

What this sector delivers

  1. Technology & knowledge dissemination Technology transfer, training and capacity building across 100+ development projects.
  2. Strategic planning & advisory Program design, project management advisory, monitoring and evaluation for 50+ organizations.
  3. HR & staffing solutions Recruitment, training and development, performance management — 500+ placements.
  4. Partnership building Facilitating collaborations between organizations, government agencies and development stakeholders.
