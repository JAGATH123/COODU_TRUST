Watershed Management

Comprehensive watershed development for soil and water conservation

Understanding Watershed Management

What is a Watershed? 🌊

A watershed is nature's water collection system - a natural drainage basin where all surface water from rain, melting snow, or ice eventually flows to a common outlet such as a river, lake, or ocean.

Watershed management is our comprehensive approach to sustainably develop and manage land and water resources within these critical geographical areas. Since 2001, Coodu Trust has pioneered integrated watershed development that transforms entire ecosystems.

Our programs focus on the holistic development of natural resources including soil, water, vegetation, and human resources within the watershed area. This integrated approach ensures sustainable utilization while dramatically improving the socio-economic conditions of communities.
