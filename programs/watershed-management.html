Watershed Management

Comprehensive watershed development for soil and water conservation

Understanding Watershed Management

Watershed management is a comprehensive approach to the sustainable development and management of land and water resources within a specific geographical area called a watershed. A watershed is a natural drainage basin where all surface water from rain, melting snow, or ice eventually flows to a common outlet such as a river, lake, or ocean.

Our watershed management programs focus on the holistic development of natural resources including soil, water, vegetation, and human resources within the watershed area. This integrated approach ensures sustainable utilization of natural resources while improving the socio-economic conditions of communities dependent on these resources.
