Understanding

What climate change adaptation means

What is climate action?

Climate change adaptation and mitigation are two complementary approaches to addressing climate challenges — adaptation strategies that build resilience, and mitigation measures that reduce emissions.

Our approach integrates both adaptation and mitigation strategies to build resilient communities and ecosystems. We focus on implementing nature-based solutions, promoting sustainable practices, and building local capacity to respond to climate variability.

Our programs emphasize ecosystem restoration and sustainable development initiatives that reduce carbon footprints while enhancing community resilience to climate impacts. Our 26,93,250 trees planted across Tamil Nadu contribute significantly to climate change mitigation through carbon absorption.

  • Climate resilience
  • Carbon sequestration
  • Water management
  • Smart agriculture

Our climate action process

  1. Climate assessment Assessing climate vulnerabilities and identifying adaptation priorities.
  2. Mitigation strategies Implementing carbon reduction measures and ecosystem restoration.
  3. Adaptation planning Developing resilience strategies for communities and ecosystems.
  4. Capacity building Training communities in climate adaptation and sustainable practices.
