Understanding Climate Change Adaptation & Mitigation

What is Climate Action? 🌡️

Climate Change Adaptation and Mitigation are two complementary approaches to addressing climate challenges through adaptation strategies and emission reduction measures.

Our approach integrates both adaptation and mitigation strategies to build resilient communities and ecosystems. We focus on implementing nature-based solutions, promoting sustainable practices, and building local capacity to respond to climate variability.

Our programs emphasize ecosystem restoration and sustainable development initiatives that reduce carbon footprints while enhancing community resilience to climate impacts.

Adaptation & Mitigation Strategies

🛡️ Adaptation Strategies

  • Drought-resistant crop varieties and farming techniques
  • Water harvesting and conservation systems
  • Early warning systems for climate risks
  • Diversified livelihood options for climate resilience
  • Ecosystem-based adaptation through habitat restoration
  • Climate-informed land use planning

🌱 Mitigation Strategies

  • Large-scale afforestation and reforestation programs
  • Sustainable agriculture and reduced chemical inputs
  • Renewable energy promotion in rural areas
  • Waste reduction and organic farming practices
  • Carbon sequestration through soil management
  • Energy-efficient technologies and practices
