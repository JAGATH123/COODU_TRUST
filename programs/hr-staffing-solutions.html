What HR & staffing solutions mean

What are HR & staffing solutions?

Human resource & staffing solutions provide end-to-end HR management services for NGOs, government programs, and development organizations — handling every aspect from recruitment to retirement.

With proven experience managing 400+ sanitation workers for Dindigul Municipal Corporation and staffing multiple large-scale programs, Coodu Trust offers comprehensive workforce solutions tailored to the development sector's unique needs — a cumulative workforce of 1,000+ professionals managed across 30 districts.

Our HR services encompass recruitment and selection, training and development, performance management, compliance and payroll, employee welfare, and workforce planning. We understand the challenges of managing diverse teams across multiple locations, seasonal staffing requirements, and the importance of maintaining mission alignment while ensuring professional management practices.

  • Recruitment & selection
  • Training & development
  • Performance management
  • Compliance & payroll
  • Employee welfare
  • Workforce planning

Our HR management approach

  1. Strategic talent acquisition Sourcing, screening and selecting right-fit professionals aligned with organizational mission and technical requirements.
  2. Continuous capacity building Structured training covering technical skills, soft skills and sector-specific knowledge through workshops, mentoring and on-the-job learning.
  3. Robust performance systems Clear KPIs, regular reviews and constructive feedback mechanisms that balance accountability with supportive development.
  4. Complete compliance management Adherence to labor laws, statutory payments (PF, ESI, PT), minimum wages and safety regulations, with full documentation and audit readiness.

Why this work matters

The challenge on the ground — and our answer

The challenge

HR overheads pull focus from the mission

Development organizations spend scarce time and capacity on recruitment, payroll and paperwork instead of the program delivery they exist for.

Our response

Full-service HR management

We handle all aspects from recruitment to retirement — payroll, appraisals, training and grievance handling — so clients can focus on program delivery.

The challenge

Large frontline workforces are hard to run well

Managing hundreds of field workers, sanitation staff and seasonal employees demands safety training, attendance tracking and welfare systems few organizations can build alone.

Our response

Workforce management at municipal scale

Bulk recruitment, safety equipment, health checkups and payroll for large teams — 400+ sanitation workers managed for Dindigul MC with full compliance and welfare measures.

The challenge

Time-bound projects can't wait for slow hiring

Government schemes, CSR projects and donor-funded programs need qualified professionals in place quickly — often across sectors and locations.

Our response

A ready talent pool for rapid deployment

We maintain a talent pool of development professionals — program managers, field officers, technical specialists and M&E analysts — enabling quick mobilization for time-bound projects.

The challenge

Compliance lapses put workers at risk

Labor law, statutory payments and documentation are complex — and failures hurt the frontline workers who can least afford it.

Our response

100% statutory compliance, dignity first

PF, ESI and PT payments, minimum wages, safety regulations and audit-ready documentation — delivered with a dignity of labor approach and worker welfare schemes.
