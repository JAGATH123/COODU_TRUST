Human Resource & Staffing Solutions

Professional HR management services for development sector organizations

Understanding HR & Staffing Solutions

Human Resource & Staffing Solutions provide end-to-end HR management services for NGOs, government programs, and development organizations. With proven experience managing 400+ sanitation workers for Dindigul Municipal Corporation and staffing multiple large-scale programs, Coodu Trust offers comprehensive workforce solutions tailored to the development sector's unique needs.

Our HR services encompass recruitment and selection, training and development, performance management, compliance and payroll, employee welfare, and workforce planning. We understand the challenges of managing diverse teams across multiple locations, seasonal staffing requirements, and the importance of maintaining mission alignment while ensuring professional management practices.

Key Components of Our HR Services:

  • Recruitment & Selection: Complete hiring process from job design to candidate placement, including advertising, screening, interviews, and reference checks
  • Training & Development: Comprehensive onboarding programs, skill development training, and continuous capacity building for staff and workers
  • Performance Management: Objective-setting frameworks, regular appraisals, feedback systems, and performance improvement plans
  • Compliance & Payroll: Labor law compliance, statutory payments, payroll processing, and documentation management
  • Employee Welfare: Health and safety programs, grievance redressal, worker welfare schemes, and benefits administration
  • Workforce Planning: Staffing forecasts, organizational structure design, and succession planning for program sustainability
