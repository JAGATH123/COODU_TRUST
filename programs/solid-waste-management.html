Solid Waste Management

Two decades of excellence in municipal waste management and zero-waste solutions

Understanding Solid Waste Management

Solid Waste Management is the systematic handling of municipal waste from collection to final disposal, ensuring environmental sustainability and public health. With 20 years of proven expertise, Coodu Trust manages comprehensive waste management operations including door-to-door collection, source segregation, transportation, composting, and final disposal.

Our programs encompass large-scale municipal operations serving tens of thousands of households, industrial waste management partnerships, and innovative zero-waste initiatives. We manage 400+ sanitation workers, operate composting facilities, and implement cutting-edge segregation technologies to achieve 70%+ efficiency rates. Our work directly contributes to cleaner cities, reduced landfill burden, and improved environmental health.

Key Components of Our Solid Waste Management:

  • Municipal Waste Operations: Comprehensive door-to-door waste collection serving 54,000+ households across 48 wards in Dindigul Municipal Corporation
  • Source Segregation: Implementation of efficient wet/dry waste segregation systems achieving 70%+ segregation rates at household level
  • Composting & Processing: Operation of ECO-MAC composting plants processing 2 tons/day of organic waste into high-quality compost
  • Industrial Partnerships: Specialized waste management for industrial areas including Renault Nissan SIPCOT facility at Padappai
  • Workforce Management: Professional management and training of 400+ sanitation workers with proper safety equipment and protocols
  • Zero-Waste Initiatives: Innovative programs targeting 100% waste diversion from landfills through recycling, composting, and reuse

Our Proven Approach to Waste Management

Coodu Trust's Solid Waste Management follows a comprehensive, scientifically-backed methodology that ensures environmental sustainability and operational efficiency.

📍

Door-to-Door Collection

Systematic daily waste collection from 54,000+ households with designated collection timings, ensuring no waste accumulation in residential areas. Our team provides color-coded bins and educates residents on proper segregation.

♻️

Source Segregation

Implementation of three-bin system (wet, dry, hazardous) at household level with continuous community education. We've achieved 70%+ segregation efficiency through consistent training and monitoring programs.

🚛

Transportation & Processing

Efficient transportation of 90 tons/day of waste using dedicated vehicles for wet and dry waste streams. Wet waste goes to composting facilities while dry waste proceeds to recycling and resource recovery centers.

🌱

Composting & Recycling

Operation of ECO-MAC composting plants converting 2 tons/day of organic waste into nutrient-rich compost for agricultural use. Dry waste is sorted, processed, and sent to authorized recyclers for material recovery.

👷

Workforce Development

Professional training and management of 400+ sanitation workers with focus on safety protocols, equipment usage, and dignity of labor. Regular health checkups and welfare programs ensure worker wellbeing.

📊

Monitoring & Reporting

Comprehensive data tracking systems for waste generation, collection efficiency, segregation rates, and processing metrics. Regular reporting to municipal authorities and stakeholders ensures transparency and accountability.

Major Solid Waste Management Projects

Dindigul Municipal Corporation

₹7.1 Crore Contract

Complete solid waste management operations for Dindigul Municipal Corporation covering 48 wards with 54,000 households. Managing 90 tons/day of municipal waste with a dedicated workforce of 400+ sanitation workers. This is one of Tamil Nadu's largest municipal waste management contracts handled by an NGO.

Project Scope:

  • Coverage: 48 municipal wards across Dindigul city
  • Households Served: 54,000+ residential units
  • Daily Waste Volume: 90 tons of mixed municipal waste
  • Workforce: 400+ sanitation workers with full training and safety equipment
  • Contract Value: ₹7.1 crore annual operations budget
  • Funder: Dindigul Municipal Corporation, Government of Tamil Nadu

Padappai SIPCOT Zero-Waste Initiative

Renault Nissan Partnership

Innovative zero-waste program for Padappai SIPCOT industrial area in partnership with Renault Nissan. Operating an ECO-MAC composting plant processing 2 tons/day with exceptional 70%+ source segregation efficiency across 3,000 households. This model demonstrates industrial-residential waste management integration.

Project Highlights:

  • Location: Padappai SIPCOT, Kanchipuram District
  • Industrial Partner: Renault Nissan India Private Limited
  • Households Covered: 3,000+ residential units in SIPCOT area
  • Composting Capacity: ECO-MAC plant processing 2 tons/day
  • Segregation Efficiency: 70%+ at source (among highest in Tamil Nadu)
  • Zero-Waste Goal: Targeting 100% waste diversion from landfills
  • Impact: Clean industrial township with minimal environmental footprint
