Understanding

What solid waste management means

What is solid waste management?

Solid waste management is the systematic handling of municipal waste from collection to final disposal — ensuring environmental sustainability and public health at every step.

With 20 years of proven expertise, Coodu Trust runs comprehensive waste operations end to end — door-to-door collection, source segregation, transportation, composting and final disposal. Our largest operation serves 54,000+ households across 48 wards of Dindigul Municipal Corporation, with a managed workforce of 400+ sanitation workers.

Alongside municipal operations, we build industrial partnerships and zero-waste initiatives — operating ECO-MAC composting plants that process 2 tons of organic waste a day and achieving 70%+ segregation at source. The result is cleaner cities, reduced landfill burden and improved environmental health.

Our waste management process

  1. Door-to-door collection Systematic daily collection from 54,000+ households with designated timings, color-coded bins and resident education on segregation.
  2. Source segregation Three-bin system (wet, dry, hazardous) at household level with continuous community education — 70%+ segregation efficiency.
  3. Transportation & processing Dedicated vehicles move 90 tons of waste a day — wet waste to composting facilities, dry waste to recycling and resource recovery centres.
  4. Composting & recycling ECO-MAC plants convert 2 tons a day of organic waste into nutrient-rich compost; sorted dry waste goes to authorised recyclers for material recovery.

Why this work matters

The challenge on the ground — and our answer

The challenge

Waste piling up in residential streets

Without reliable collection, municipal waste accumulates in residential areas — threatening public health across whole city wards.

Our response

Door-to-door collection at city scale

Daily collection from 54,000+ households across 48 wards of Dindigul Municipal Corporation — 90 tons of municipal waste managed every day.

The challenge

Mixed waste heads straight to landfill

When wet, dry and hazardous waste travel together, nothing can be recovered — and the landfill burden keeps growing.

Our response

Segregation at the source

A three-bin system backed by continuous training and monitoring delivers 70%+ segregation efficiency — among the highest in Tamil Nadu — with zero-waste programmes targeting 100% diversion from landfills.

The challenge

Organic waste dumped instead of used

Wet waste that could feed farmland rots in dumps — wasted as a resource and polluting the environment.

Our response

Composting waste into value

ECO-MAC composting plants process 2 tons of organic waste a day — 730+ tons of high-quality compost a year for agricultural use.

The challenge

Sanitation work without safety or dignity

Waste work is too often done without proper equipment, training or care for the people who do it.

Our response

A trained, protected workforce

400+ sanitation workers with professional training, safety equipment, regular health checkups and welfare programmes — dignity of labor built in.
