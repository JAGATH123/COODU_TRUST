Women Empowerment & Livelihoods

Empowering women as catalysts of social and economic transformation

Women Leading Change

Women empowerment is at the heart of our development philosophy. We believe that when women are empowered economically and socially, entire communities transform. Our comprehensive approach includes forming self-help groups, developing micro-enterprises, building leadership skills, and addressing gender inequalities through community-based interventions.
