Environment & Climate Change

Building climate resilience and environmental sustainability

Protecting Our Planet

Environmental sustainability and climate resilience are crucial for long-term community development. Our environmental programs focus on renewable energy promotion, environmental education, coastal area protection, and climate adaptation strategies. We work to build awareness about environmental issues while implementing practical solutions that communities can adopt and sustain.

Success Stories

"From Darkness to Solar Light"

"Our village was one of the last to get electricity connection. The solar energy program not only provided us with reliable power but also reduced our electricity bills by 70%. Now we have street lights and our children can study at night."

Ramaswamy Village Theni District

"Mangroves: Our Natural Shield"

"The mangrove restoration saved our village from coastal erosion. What was once a barren coastline is now a thriving ecosystem. Fish have returned to our waters and our homes are protected from storm surges."

Coastal Community Nagapattinam District
