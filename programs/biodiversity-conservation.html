Understanding Biodiversity Conservation

What is Biodiversity Conservation? 🌿

Biodiversity Conservation refers to the protection, preservation, and sustainable management of biological diversity including ecosystems, species, and genetic resources.

Our approach focuses on protecting critical ecosystems like the Western Ghats, conserving medicinal plants, and promoting ecological preservation through sustainable development practices. We recognize that biodiversity is essential for ecosystem stability, environmental health, and human well-being.

Our programs integrate traditional knowledge with modern conservation science to create holistic solutions that protect natural habitats, preserve endangered species, and support ecological balance for future generations.

Western Ghats Development Programme (WGDP)

Coodu Trust implemented the Western Ghats Development Programme (WGDP) in Virudhunagar District, a government initiative focused on protecting one of the world's most important biodiversity hotspots while promoting sustainable development.

The Western Ghats represent a UNESCO World Heritage Site with high levels of endemism and critical ecosystems. Our conservation work in this region included:

  • GIS Mapping: Digitized 421 villages in Virudhunagar district for spatial planning and conservation management
  • Forest Department Partnership: Collaborated with Tamil Nadu Forest Department in Sivagangai district for biodiversity protection
  • Ecosystem Protection: Protected endemic flora and fauna unique to the Western Ghats
  • Sustainable Development: Promoted eco-friendly livelihood options that respect ecological limits
  • Traditional Knowledge: Preserved indigenous ecological knowledge of local communities
  • Habitat Conservation: Maintained forest cover and prevented habitat fragmentation

WGDP Programme Highlights

📍 Geographic Coverage

Virudhunagar District: Primary implementation area with comprehensive village-level GIS mapping covering 421 villages for spatial planning and biodiversity monitoring.

🤝 Institutional Partnership

Tamil Nadu Forest Department: Strategic collaboration in Sivagangai district for integrated biodiversity conservation and sustainable forest management.

🌳 Conservation Activities

Invasive species removal (Prosopis Juliflora), habitat restoration, medicinal plant conservation, and endemic species protection within the Western Ghats biodiversity hotspot.

💻 Technology Integration

Advanced GIS mapping and spatial data management for 421 villages, enabling evidence-based conservation planning and sustainable resource management.
