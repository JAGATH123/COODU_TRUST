Water Resource Management

Rainwater harvesting, water conservation, and sustainable water management systems

Understanding Water Resource Management

Water Resource Management is the comprehensive planning, development, distribution, and optimization of water resources to meet current and future human and environmental needs. It involves the systematic management of water through various technologies, policies, and practices to ensure sustainable water security for communities and ecosystems.

Our water resource management programs focus on sustainable water conservation, rainwater harvesting, groundwater recharge, and community-based water management systems. We emphasize participatory approaches that involve local communities in water resource planning and management, ensuring long-term sustainability and equitable water distribution.

Key Components of Water Resource Management:

  • Rainwater Harvesting: Collecting and storing rainwater for various uses including groundwater recharge and irrigation
  • Water Conservation: Implementing efficient water use practices and reducing water wastage through technology and awareness
  • Groundwater Recharge: Enhancing natural groundwater replenishment through recharge shafts and percolation systems
  • Tank Desilting: Restoring water storage capacity of traditional water bodies through desilting and rehabilitation
  • Participatory Management: Involving water user associations and communities in sustainable water management practices
  • Ecosystem Restoration: Rejuvenating water bodies and surrounding ecosystems for biodiversity conservation
