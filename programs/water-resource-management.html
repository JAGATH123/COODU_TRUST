Water Resource Management

Rainwater harvesting, water conservation, and sustainable water management systems

Understanding Water Resource Management

What is Water Resource Management? 💧

Water Resource Management is the comprehensive planning, development, distribution, and optimization of water resources to meet current and future human and environmental needs through systematic water management practices.

Our water resource management programs focus on sustainable water conservation, rainwater harvesting, groundwater recharge, and community-based water management systems. Since 2000, Coodu Trust has pioneered innovative water management solutions that transform water security across rural communities.

We emphasize participatory approaches that involve local communities in water resource planning and management, ensuring long-term sustainability and equitable water distribution while building climate resilience.

Our Water Management Process

Resource Assessment

Rainwater Harvesting

3

Water Body Restoration

Tank desilting, water body rehabilitation, and ecosystem restoration for enhanced water storage.

4

Community Management

Capacity building of water user associations and sustainable water management training.
