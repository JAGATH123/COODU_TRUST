Environment and Resilience

Building climate-resilient communities through environmental conservation and sustainable development

Creating a Sustainable Future

For over two decades, Coodu Trust has been at the forefront of environmental conservation and climate resilience in Tamil Nadu. Our comprehensive approach addresses the interconnected challenges of climate change, environmental degradation, and sustainable development through science-based solutions and community empowerment.

We integrate traditional ecological wisdom with cutting-edge environmental science to create lasting solutions. From watershed restoration and biodiversity conservation to renewable energy adoption and climate adaptation strategies, our programs build resilient ecosystems that support both nature and human communities.

Our Environmental Mission

  • 🌱 Restore degraded ecosystems through scientific watershed management
  • 💧 Secure water futures with sustainable resource management
  • 🌳 Enhance biodiversity through large-scale afforestation programs
  • 🏘️ Build climate resilience in vulnerable rural communities
  • ⚡ Promote clean energy and sustainable development practices
