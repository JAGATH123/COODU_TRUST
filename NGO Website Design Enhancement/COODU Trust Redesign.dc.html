Programs Women Empowerment

Women Empowerment

Empowering women as catalysts of social and economic transformation.

Program overview

Creating sustainable change through women

Women empowerment is at the heart of sustainable development. Our program recognises women as key agents of change — and through social empowerment, financial inclusion, entrepreneurship and community mobilisation, we create pathways to economic independence, recognition and leadership.

We form and strengthen Self-Help Groups, provide microfinance, build entrepreneurial and leadership skills, and foster women's participation in decision-making — consistently demonstrating that empowered women drive poverty reduction, better education and stronger communities.

Our impact

Women empowerment impact

Leadership Development

Over 2,000 women have emerged as community leaders in local governance, SHGs and development committees.

Economic Independence

800+ women-led micro-enterprises generate sustainable income and create jobs for other women.

Financial Inclusion

450+ SHGs have mobilised savings exceeding ₹50 lakhs, opening access to credit and financial services.

Social Transformation

Empowered women drive better education, health and quality of life for their families and future generations.

Community Solidarity

Strong women's collectives form support networks that promote gender equality and advocate for women's rights.

Sustainable Development

Women's participation leads to more inclusive, sustainable outcomes benefiting entire communities.
